    Sup

    A superior non-magical base: more enhanced damage or defense than a plain one (a red or purple star when it's among the best)

    Eth

    Ethereal: more damage or defense, but it can't be repaired

    [Larzuk]

    A base Larzuk can give 4 sockets (his quest reward), at your character level

    [Imbue]

    A base worth imbuing with Charsi's quest reward: it becomes a rare item

    ($)

    Worth selling: it sells for a lot of gold at your character level

    Worth selling, less: 10,000 to 19,999 gold below character level 60

    $12k

    Its sell price in thousands of gold (12k = 12,000), in Normal below character level 30

    LLD

    Good for Low Level Dueling (PvP at character level 30 and below)

    How to read a tag
    • The number is the item's own roll (or its total, a roll plus its runes): 20fcr on a ring is 20% faster cast rate.
    • The letters say which stat it is; the card's title writes the number as #. Some letters mean more than one stat and the color tells them apart: #r is fire resistance in red, cold in blue, lightning in yellow, and poison in green.
    • The color: where a tag comes in more than one color for a kind of item, the color says how good the roll is. Each card draws the tag in each of its looks at the lowest value that look takes, with the values beside it (30+: from 30; 10–29; any: at any value).
    • The kinds of item above a tag all show it that way; where the color or the values differ, a kind has a line of its own.
    • Tags show once an item is identified.
