    At a glance

    Skip to the bases, base by base

    What each kind of magic item does at each filter level. Filter levels that treat them the same share a column. from clvl 80 means from character level 80; the strip under a cell shows character levels 1 to 99, hatched where items hide. Click a cell to see its bases at that filter level below.

    Swipe the table sideways for more filter levels.

    Base by base

    How to read this page: the rows, the columns, and the filter levels

    How to read it

    How the Erazure loot filter shows magic items that drop on the ground, unidentified, read from the filter's own rules. At the filter levels a base's rows don't cover, it shows with its plain name in blue, with Eth before it when it is ethereal.

    Notify minimap icon and chat notification, and a drop sound where one is shown Shows as a look of its own (shown as the game draws it) Hidden the item is not shown
    • Each row is one case: the filter levels it applies to, what happens, and what the item needs. Every column must hold; any means no condition. Pick your filter level in the bar to see only your rows.
    • Crafted affix level is the affix level the filter works out for a craft made from the item: from half your character level plus half its item level.
    • Bases of one type and tier with the same socket maximum and rows share a group. A look shows the group's first base; each base shows its own name.
    • Hidden items still show in town. The minimap icon and chat notification come on their own, even for a hidden item.
    • Charms drop only as magic items (the game has no rare charms). The number after the dot in a charm's look is its item level; a row shows its lowest.
