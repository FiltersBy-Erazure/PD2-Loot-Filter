    Examples of Stylization and Aesthetic¶

    How the filter looks in game. The pictures of items on the ground are Erazure - Main on filter level 1 (potions, scrolls, and tomes for a level 5 character, everything else for level 90); the hover boxes are screenshots from the game. Tap or click a label in a picture of items on the ground to see which filter levels show it and to hear its drop sound; anywhere else, to see the picture full size. Versions shows the same drops in each version.

    Unique and set items¶

    Unidentified on the ground, a unique or set item's look tells its tier; Unique & Set Tiers has every item's.

    Left, High value: the bases of Harlequin Crest, Tyrael's Might, Griffon's Eye, and Dracul's Grasp. Middle, Medium value (Lightsabre or Azurewrath, The Grandfather) and Minimal value (Blood Raven's Charge, Lycander's Flank). Right, a Diablo Clone or Rathma unique (Cage of the Unsullied), a unique in no tier, the Grail look of one you haven't discovered yet, and a unique for low level dueling.

    Left, High value: Tal Rasha's Guardianship, Natalya's Mark, and Griswold's Honor. Middle, Medium value: M'avina's True Sight, Guillaume's Face, and Aldur's Deception. Right, the standard set look, the dimmer look of a low set item at a high character level, and the Grail look of one you haven't discovered yet.

    Tags and highlights¶

    Once an item is identified, the line under its name holds the filter's tags: its best stats in short, 30fcr, 45r, and 3mpk (Tags and Symbols explains each). A unique's or set item's tags are its rolls. A line between stars is a highlight (Skill & Shop Highlights lists those on jewelry, armor, weapons, and class items), and notes such as Max Sockets and Upgrade, with its recipe, come under the name too.     Maps¶

    An identified map's hover box opens with its tags (experience, monster rarity, monster density, and magic find), then its area level, the highest resistances and the immunities of its monsters, its guest monsters, and its mods to watch out for: gray for a small roll, red for a dangerous one. A high monster rarity or density roll stands between stars, more of them the higher it is. Under them come its experience lines: on an uncorrupted map the tag, 1.80x Experience, is how much more a full clear gives with the map's rolls than without them (is it worth corrupting?), and on every map 2.10x at Level 90 is what it gives you at your level against the average plain T1-T3 map, for the same distance walked. Map Experience explains both. These pictures are from before November 17th, when both numbers had one decimal, corrupted maps showed the tag too, and the lines came in another order.
