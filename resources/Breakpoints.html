Good to know

  • Frames are the game's, 25 a second: 9 frames is 0.36 seconds.
  • Each stat is diminished: what counts is 120 × your % ÷ (120 + your %), so every breakpoint needs more than the one before.
  • Cast rate stops at 75 of that (about 200% faster cast rate): past the last row, more does nothing.
  • Chilled (by cold damage), you lose up to 50% faster cast rate while it lasts.
  • Holy Shield doubles a Paladin's block speed: 86% faster block rate is then 1 frame.
  • A map's −% hit recovery comes off your faster hit recovery: Map Lines says when its line shows for you.
