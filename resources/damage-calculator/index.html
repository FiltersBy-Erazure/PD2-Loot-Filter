    Damage Calculator¶

    Season 14 · Physical damage · Attack speed

    Compare up to four physical builds against any monster: damage per second, kill time, attack speed breakpoints and what each stat is worth.

    The monster

    Builds

    Pick an example in Start from… on a card, then put in your own numbers. Under each part of a build, what it makes; at the bottom of the screen, every build’s damage per second.

    Fields marked ● gold differ from build A (on builds of A’s class).

    How they compare

    Side by side

    Every number behind each build’s damage per second. The best of a row is marked.

    Average hit: the damage a hit does on average, with critical and deadly strikes counted at their chance. Damage per second counts every hit an attack makes; crushing blow is in the kill time only, since it takes a share of the monster’s life left.

    Attack speed breakpoints

    The increased attack speed each build needs on its other gear for each frame count of its attack (its weapon’s IAS as set; its skills’ attack speed and its weapon’s speed counted), and its damage per second there.

    Stat trade-offs

    +%

    Flat damage counts the same number added to minimum and to maximum damage. A stat a build can’t gain this way shows a dash (critical and deadly strike stop at their caps).

    Damage profile

    Every hit’s damage against the monster, sorted (the hits that land: a miss does none): the left end is the weakest hit, the right end the strongest. Deadly and critical strikes make the steps on the right.

      How it’s worked out

      • The weapon: an ethereal weapon has 5/4 of its base damage, rounded down. Enhanced damage on the weapon (its own, a superior base’s, its sockets’) raises only the base damage: base + base × enhanced damage / 100, rounded down; then its flat minimum and maximum damage are added.
      • The attack: the weapon’s damage plus flat damage from other gear, times (100 + the attack skill’s damage + your auras’ and buffs’ damage + Strength × the weapon’s Strength bonus / 100 + Dexterity × its Dexterity bonus / 100 + your weapon mastery + damage against demons or undead when the monster is one + enhanced damage from other gear) / 100; a melee hit with a blunt weapon (a mace, hammer, club, scepter, wand or staff) adds 50 more against undead, an arrow or a thrown weapon doesn’t. Your character screen leaves the part against demons or undead out. A minimum below 1 becomes 1 and a maximum at or below the minimum becomes the minimum + 1, before the percentages. The game keeps it in 256ths of a point; a hit is any damage from the minimum to the maximum alike. A skill that deals part of the weapon’s damage (Stun, Guided Arrow, Blade Fury) takes that part.
      • Your own aura gives you its full damage bonus; a party member’s or your mercenary’s gives half (the “Party Damage” of the tooltip).
      • Critical strike comes first: chance (Amazon’s Critical Strike, items, a mastery) at most 75%, double damage plus the critical strike multiplier. Only a hit that isn’t critical can be a deadly strike: chance at most 75% plus maximum deadly strike, 1.5 times damage plus the deadly strike multiplier.
      • Physical resistance: the monster’s resistance plus a curse, Amplify Damage or Decrepify (Weaken and Attract leave it), and Battle Cry, which is no curse and goes with any. A monster holds one curse and one Battle Cry at a time, so what a build puts on it itself (Curses and debuffs on its card: its own skill, charges, a chance to cast on striking, Call to Arms’ Battle Cry) replaces the party’s (the monster’s fields), which every other build keeps. Against a monster whose own resistance is 100% or more, curses and Battle Cry count half. Physical pierce lowers what is left below 100%, and below 0% it counts half. Damage is lowered by the resistance (raised at negative resistance, at most double).
      • Chance to hit: every hit rolls on its own, and only a hit that lands does damage or a crushing blow. Attack rating = (5 × Dexterity − 35 + the class’s own (Amazon and Druid 5, Assassin 15, Barbarian and Paladin 20, Necromancer −10, Sorceress −15) + attack rating on gear and from Grim Ward, + attack rating against demons or undead when the monster is one) × (100 + attack rating % on gear + from your auras and buffs (Fanaticism, Concentration, Blessed Aim, Heart of Wolverine, your own Holy aura, a wereform’s by Lycanthropy’s level, Penetrate’s level) + the attack skill’s own + your weapon mastery’s on a melee hit) / 100, rounded down. Your character screen shows it with the attack on a mouse button, without the part against demons or undead; with a throw skill and a throwing weapon it adds Throwing Mastery’s, which a thrown hit doesn’t get. The monster’s defense (a boss’s in Hell at its own level) is first lowered in points by Inner Sight and Attract, then by Conviction, Battle Cry (at most 30%) and Weaken, the three added up; below 0 it adds to your attack rating. Ignore Target’s Defense makes it 0 against a normal monster, a champion or a minion, not against a unique, superunique, boss or player; −% target defense (your gear’s, and your own Blessed Aim’s, its base points) lowers it by that share, half against a superunique, a boss or a player, at most 100%. Chance to hit = 2 × 111 × attack rating / (attack rating + defense) × your level / (your level + its level), at least 5%; above 95% what is left of the 111 counts 90/111, at most 100%, every step rounded down. Smite and Guided Arrow never miss; Bash, Concentrate, Feral Rage and Maul roll as the swing starts, and their hit lands only if that roll did.
      • Crushing blow takes a share of the life the monster has left, whatever its life: 1/8 for a monster (champions and uniques too), 1/30 for a map boss, 1/10 for a player or a pet, and 1/70 for another boss at full life, less as it loses life (1/(70 + 10 for every 1% it is missing)). Each player after the first adds 1.4 to the 8, 30 or 70 (their 70% more life, / 50). A ranged attack divides by 1.5 times as much; crushing blow efficiency multiplies the share by (1 + efficiency / 100). The monster’s physical resistance lowers it (none at 100% or more; pierce doesn’t count). More chance and more efficiency are always better; the kill time shows how much, beside the time without it.
      • Frames per attack come from the IAS Calculator, which has the whole model and plays each attack frame by frame: speed = 100 + the skills’ speed (Fanaticism, Frenzy by its charges, Burst of Speed, Werewolf, the attack’s own) − the weapon’s speed modifier + effective IAS (120 × IAS / (120 + IAS)), 30 less for a sequence, kept between 15 and 175; an animation of N frames takes N × 256 / (its speed × speed / 100) frames, rounded up, minus 1, held. A normal attack is attack 1 or attack 2, half the time each, so it counts both, and it hits with the first weapon only. Two weapons: only the faster one’s speed counts. Zeal, Fend, Fury, Strafe and Dragon Talon rewind their animation between hits (Strafe: its fewest arrows, at one monster); Whirlwind and Blade Dance hit every 6 frames, with each weapon, whatever the attack speed, slows or Frenzy’s charges; Charge’s strike runs at a speed the game sets when it connects, and its damage per second and kill time count the strikes back to back (the run before each strike isn’t counted). A druid’s wereform attacks run at a speed set by the weapon’s attack speed in human form.
      • Next breakpoint: the increased attack speed a build still needs on its other gear (its weapon’s IAS as set) for fewer frames, and what that is worth in damage per second. A build at the most its attack counts shows none.
      • The bosses have their life, defense and physical resistance in Hell: the life of the boss’s own level, +70% for each player after the first (at most 8,388,607). Diablo Clone, Rathma, Mendeln and Lucion have 1.5 times that at tier 0, 4.8 times at tier 1 and 5.6 times at tier 2; their phases, immunity, healing and helpers aren’t counted. The map and dungeon bosses on the game’s map boss lists take crushing blow as map bosses (1/30 of their life); The Tainted Hive, Na-Krul, the two Djinns and Zoltun Kulle aren’t on them and take it as other bosses do. Map modifiers aren’t counted.
      • How they compare: damage per second is the average hit × what critical and deadly strikes add × what the monster’s resistance lets through × hits per second × the chance to hit, so a build’s difference from build A splits exactly into those five. On a build of build A’s class, the fields that differ from build A are marked gold (across classes nearly every field would be).
      • Skills that read another skill’s level: Dragon Claw’s damage counts the level of Claw and Dagger Mastery as its tooltip does; Penetrate’s base points are both Strafe’s synergy and its pierce.
      • Not counted: elemental, poison and magic damage, open wounds, life leech, the monster’s blocking, map modifiers, splash damage to other monsters, mercenaries and summons.
