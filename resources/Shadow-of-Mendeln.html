Four choices make it bigger

Fortified

Every kill counts twice, the event's too. A Fortified map has half as many monsters of its own, each worth twice as much, so its clear gives about what the same map gives without it. The event isn't halved: on a Fortified map it is worth twice as much.

A party

Each player in the game after the first adds 50% to every monster's experience, and your party shares every kill near it. With 8 players each of you gets times what one player alone would. It makes no difference who gets the kills.

A higher tier

The waves are as high as the map's area level: 87 in T1, 88 in T2, and 89 in T3. Up to level 92 that changes almost nothing. From there your level takes experience off monsters 6 or more levels below you: a T1 map's from level 93, a T2 map's from 94, a T3 map's from 95. The higher the map, the less it loses.

+1 area level

One of a map corruption's outcomes, 9% of them. A T1 map with it gives what a T2 map does, a T2 map what a T3 map does, and a T3 map with it (area level 90) is the most there is. Fortify a corrupted map: the Horadric Cube takes it.

    Good to know

    • Who gets the kills makes no difference. Party members within about 53 yards of a kill share it, and the waves come within about 7 yards of Mendeln, so everyone in your party at the event shares every kill.
    • Players: each player in the game after the first adds 50% to every monster's experience when it spawns, and 70% to its life.
    • How often: he shows up in about 1 rare map in 26, 1 magic map in 53, and 1 non-magical map in 82.
    • No loot from the waves: the event's loot is Mendeln's own drop when he leaves.
    • Nothing else on the map changes it: Monster Density, Monster Rarity, "Map contains ..." monsters, Treacherous, and the other monster mods. Every event is the same 45 waves.
    • Fortified's price: the waves get the map's monster mods, Fortified's twice the life and +40% damage among them, so the event takes longer and hits harder.
    • Mendeln himself can't be attacked and gives no experience. Between waves he casts curses.
    • At level 99 there's no experience to gain.
    How it's worked out
    • The waves. Waves 1–20 bring 0–7 normal monsters each; waves 21–35 0–5 normal monsters and 0–2 champions; waves 36–45 0–3 normal monsters, 0–1 champion, and 0–2 uniques, each unique with 3–6 minions. On average that is . Four in five are Hungry Dead, Defiled Warriors, Horrors, or Horror Archers, one in five a Horror Mage; all give the same experience.
    • Each monster's experience. The same for every wave monster of a map: , for one player. Champions give 3 times as much and are 2 levels higher; 1 in 5 is a berserker, 5 times as much and 3 levels higher. Uniques give 5 times as much and are 3 levels higher. A unique's minions give the plain experience, at the map's level.
    • Players and your party. Each player in the game after the first adds 50% when the monster spawns. A kill is shared by the party members within about 53 yards: together they get 1 + 0.35 for each member after the first, split between them by level (this page takes everyone at your level).
    • Your level. A monster up to 5 levels below you gives its full experience, then 81%, 62%, 43%, and 24% at 6, 7, 8, and 9 levels below, and 5% from 10 levels below. A monster above your level gives your level divided by its level. From level 70 a character gets only part of every kill's experience, less the higher it is: . Then your +% experience (your own and the map's add up).

    • Full clears. The tier's average map from the model the Map Experience page explains: every monster a full clear of each map spawns, and its level.
