    Monster Damage Calculator¶

    How hard does one hit of a map monster land on you? Pick the monster or one of the map's bosses (its melee hits, or a boss's spells), its mods and auras, the map's mods and a curse on you: the page works out the hit, which parts add up and which multiply. Season 14. Nearly every monster's hits can also deal double damage (5%), so that is always counted.

    The monster

    Example setup

    Unique monster mods

    Melee hits, and the bosses' spells and missiles (under Attack). Other monsters' spells and missiles aren't counted here.

    Auras and curses

    The levels follow the map: an aura or a curse that comes from a unique's mod is at the unique's level, which grows with the map's area level, so it changes with the tier. A monster casts its own curses at the same level in every tier. Type a level to use your own. One curse at a time. Curse Resistance (at most 75%) lowers what a curse does; Conviction is an aura, so it doesn't lower that.

      How It Adds Up

      A dashed step adds into a pool; a solid one multiplies. Numbers are for an average roll.

      The Rules Behind It

      • +% damage adds up, then multiplies the physical part once. One pool holds the map's Enhanced Physical Damage, Fortified's 40, the elite bonus (Hell: champion 66, Berserker 198), Extra Strong (unique 99, its minions 49) and Might, Concentration or Fanaticism.
      • Deadly strike multiplies the physical part by 1.5, after that pool. It never touches elemental damage.
      • Many monsters' attacks carry elemental damage of their own, on every hit or on a share of them, which grows with the monster's level and the players in the game as its physical damage does. Spectral Hit's element takes the place of the attack's own damage of that element.
      • Elemental damage of one kind is one roll. "Physical damage as extra", the attack's own, Fire, Cold and Lightning Enchanted, Holy Fire and Holy Shock's added damage and Spectral Hit all add to it; then the skill damage mods (Flaming, Hibernal, Static, Toxic and Fortified's) raise it. +% damage and deadly strike don't.
      • "Physical damage as extra" is a share of the monster's base attack, before the +% pool and deadly strike. The poison version's number is per frame: over its 5 seconds it deals 125/256 of what it says (163% shown is about 80%).
      • Enchanted uniques add 66–100% of their level's damage figure to their attacks in that element; their minions 33–50%.
      • Spectral Hit adds 66–100% of the unique's level damage figure to each attack in one random element: fire, lightning, magic, cold or poison (1 attack in 5 each). Its poison is a per-frame figure too: alone it deals 40/256 of it over 1.6 seconds. On a map that adds poison, a hit with both lasts 290 frames instead of 125, so all of its poison deals 290/256 of its figure.
      • Aura Enchanted gives a unique one of eight monster auras (Conviction, Fanaticism, Holy Shock, Holy Freeze, Holy Fire, Might, Concentration, Vigor) at its level / 7, at most 13: a unique is 3 levels above the area, so that's level 12 in T1 maps and 13 in T2 and up (or with +1 area level). Holy Fire, Holy Freeze and Holy Shock add their damage to the unique's own attacks; Vigor changes no hit numbers here.
      • Cursed casts Amplify Damage on 3 of a unique's hits in 4, at its level / 5 + 1: level 19 in T1–T3 maps, 20 in T4 dungeons.
      • Some bosses have an aura of their own, always on, and its damage joins their attacks: Son of Rakkis's Holy Shock (level 15) and Shattered Soul's Holy Freeze (level 10).
      • The 5% double comes last and doubles everything in the hit. With deadly strike too, the physical part is 3x. It's each monster's own chance (5% for nearly all; T4 dungeons' Blood Bringers, the Outer Void's Void Knights and the bosses Gregus Mazi, Overlord Aerlynn and Warlord of Blood have none), and it doubles spells and missiles as well as melee hits.
      • A boss's spells deal their own damage, at their skill level (7 higher in Hell than in Normal): the monster's level and the players in the game don't change it. The map's +% fire, cold, lightning and poison skill damage and Fortified's raise its elemental part, as they do the attack's; a few missiles carry damage of their own that they don't raise. The +% damage pool, deadly strike, "physical damage as extra" and the attack's own elemental damage only reach the spells that strike with a share of the boss's attack (that share of each).
      • Fire on the ground (a fire wall, the burning ground a meteor or a blaze leaves) hits again and again while you stand in it, up to 25 times a second, and takes its share of your flat damage reduction each time: the page shows a second standing in it.
      • Fortified adds 40 to the +% damage pool, though the orb's text says +20%.
      • Curses and Conviction lower your totals, the same way the map's -resist does. Your physical damage reduction counts up to 50% against a hit and your resistances up to your maximum (at most 90%), so anything above that soaks them first. Amplify Damage and Decrepify lower physical damage reduction; Lower Resist lowers fire, cold, lightning and poison; Conviction fire, cold and lightning.
      • Crushing blow ignores the hit's damage: it takes a tenth of your current life, less your whole physical damage reduction (curses included, not capped at 50%). At 100% or more it does nothing.
      • Flat damage reduction comes off physical damage before the percentage; magic damage reduction off fire, cold, lightning and magic. Poison ignores both.
      • Each player after the first adds 6.25% to monsters' base attack damage (and 70% to their life).
      • Not counted here: how often you're hit (attack rating, defense, block, attack speed), the pulse damage of the auras a monster gets from a unique mod or a monster near it (Holy Fire, Holy Shock, Holy Freeze), open wounds, absorb, poison length reduction, how many of a spell's bolts or shards reach you, and the spells and missiles of monsters other than bosses.
