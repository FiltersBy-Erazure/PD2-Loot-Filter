    LLD Item Guide¶

    Season 14 · Gear · Low level dueling

    Plan a low level dueling item for level 30, 18, or 9: every way to make it, ranked, and the item level to aim for.

    How to use this guide
    1. Pick your bracket and class in Your bracket: level 30, 18, or 9. Some affixes need a lower level for one class.
    2. Say what you want: start from an item Erazure tags LLD, or pick a base and the affixes you want (type a stat, like resist or life). If you'll corrupt it, tick the corruptions you'd like under Then corrupt it.
    3. Read the ways to make it, likeliest first: each row is a way, with its chance per item (rare rings found, crafts, rerolls) and the item level it's at. The box under them says the item level to aim for and how to get there: where monsters of that level are, or the character level and item to craft with.
    4. Use the chart to see where each affix can roll and the chance at every item level: ★ marks the best, ◇ what's about as good. Click, tap, or use the arrow keys to pick another item level; the box follows. You choose the item level (where it drops, or what you craft with); its affix level follows from it. How the game works is under the guide.

    On a phone the answer is at the top; what it's for is under it.

    How it works

    item level, affixes, and the ways to make an item

    From item level to the level it needs

    1. Its item level. An item's item level is the same as the monster level of the monster that dropped it. A crafted item's item level comes from your character level and the item level of the item you put in, each part rounded down: Crafted item level = your character level ÷ 2 + the item's item level ÷ 2 For example, a level 30 character crafting with an item of item level 72 makes crafted item level 15 + 36 = 51. Rerolling a rare item with 3 Perfect Skulls gives the new rare 40% of each instead (so at most 78). Rerolling a magic item with 3 perfect gems keeps its item level.
    2. How many affixes it rolls.

A craft's affixes come besides its fixed mods, and its lowest count is the likeliest: 1 affix 40% of the time up to item level 30, 2 affixes 60% at 31-50, and 3 affixes 80% at 51-70. A magic item has 1 or 2 (both a quarter of the time), always 2 from item level 65 (85 on rings, amulets, and jewels, 90 on charms).
    3. Its affix level. The item level minus half the base's quality level, rounded down, catching up near item level 99: the same as the item level on a Ring or an Amulet, 1 lower on Leather Gloves, and 14 lower on a Small Charm up to item level 85, then catching up: 81 at 90, 99 at 99.
    4. Which affixes can roll. Each affix only between its own lowest and highest affix level. The higher the affix level, the stronger the affixes that can roll and the more of them need more than your bracket, while the weakest stop at their highest.
    5. The level it needs. The highest of: the base itself (a Diadem needs 64, so it can never be an LLD item), each of its prefixes and suffixes, on magic and rare items its automagic (an orb's life, a Paladin shield's resistances, or an Amazon weapon's skill tab: the stronger ones need more than 30), each class skill on it, and whatever is socketed in it. Some affixes need less for characters of one class (the charge suffixes), so pick your class above.

    The ways to make an item

    Drops

    A dropped item's item level is the monster level of the monster that dropped it: champions are 2 levels above their area's monsters, unique monsters 3.

    The lower the item level, the fewer strong affixes can roll, and the fewer of them need more than your bracket.

    The kills count every monster you'd kill clearing the area, its champions and unique monsters as often as they spawn, for one player, with the magic find you enter (a map adds its own). Magic find raises the chance of a rare, not of a magic ring, amulet, charm, or jewel. A party of three or more near you makes ordinary monsters drop about 10% more.

    Crafting

    A magic or rare item of the craft's type, with the recipe's ingredients or its craft infusion, becomes a crafted item of the same base: the craft's fixed mods plus 1 to 4 affixes of its own. Its old affixes don't carry over, but an ethereal item makes an ethereal craft. Crafted item levels go up to 98.

    Crafting adds nothing to the level it needs, and neither do the fixed mods (Diablo II added 10, plus 3 per affix; Project Diablo 2 doesn't).

    Rerolling a magic item with 3 perfect gems

    Used mostly on magic Small Charms and Grand Charms. It rerolls the item's affixes and keeps its base and item level, so each try is as likely to give what you want as a drop of that item level. You can reroll as often as you have gems.

    Buying from a vendor

    In Normal difficulty a store's items have your character level + 5 as their item level, at most 12, 20, 28, 36, and 45 in Acts 1 to 5, so a character of any level higher shops at that act's most. A vendor sells magic copies of a base only from an item level of its own, and non-magical ones below 25.

    Stores sell magic armor and weapons (claws, daggers, scepters, wands, and staves among them, and quivers), but no rings, amulets, jewels, charms, circlets, orbs, Amazon weapons, or class helms and shields. Nightmare and Hell stores sell upgraded bases instead, so the guide counts Normal's.

    Rerolling a rare item with 3 Perfect Skulls

    Used mostly on rare jewels. A new rare of the same base, at 40% of your character level plus 40% of the item's item level, each rounded down (so at most 78). It is never ethereal.

    Each try costs 3 Perfect Skulls, so it rarely pays for LLD gear.

    Bricking with a Worldstone Shard

    Corrupting an item with no sockets can turn it into a rare of the same base and item level: half the time for a non-magical weapon, armor, or quiver, and a quarter of the time for a magic item, rings and amulets included. Each try costs a shard and the item.

    The rare comes corrupted, with a corruption of its own: one of a rare's corruptions other than turning into another rare, each a third likelier than when you corrupt one. Erazure's LLD tags count a point for an uncorrupted item, so a bricked one needs one point more, or a corruption that gives it.
