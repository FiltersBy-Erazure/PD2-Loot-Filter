Good to know

  • One try. A corrupted item can't be corrupted again, and nothing takes a corruption off.
  • Sockets. An item that gets sockets gets as many as the outcome says, at most what its base can hold at its item level.
  • Turns into a rare. The item is gone: a new rare item of the same base takes its place, with new random affixes. On a non-magical item that is often the point of corrupting it (bricking).
  • Desecrated amulets (from Lucion) can turn rare; how likely depends on Lucion's tier. Otherwise they get one of the amulets' rarest outcomes: +1 to All Skills, +10% Faster Cast Rate, +10% Faster Run/Walk, Curse Resistance +10%, +30-40% Enhanced Damage, Cannot Be Frozen, +2% to all Maximum Resistances, or All Resistances +7-10.
  • Maps corrupt with their own odds: each outcome adds its map stats, and the rest of the roll turns the map into a unique map. The lines the filter writes under a map's name for those stats, and when each shows: Map Lines.
