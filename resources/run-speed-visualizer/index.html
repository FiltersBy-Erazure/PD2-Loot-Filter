    Run Speed Visualizer¶

    Season 14 · Faster run/walk · Movement skills

    How fast you run (or whirlwind) with your faster run/walk (FRW) and your skills, up to four setups side by side. FRW on gear has diminishing returns; the movement your skills give doesn’t. The chart shows what more FRW on gear would still bring.

    Attack speed breakpoints for setup A's class: the IAS Calculator.

    See them run

    Each setup from a standing start, its buffs just cast, at the game’s own speed and scale. The legs move as fast as the game moves them. A buff that runs out on the way (Blaze, Joust) stops counting there.

      Side by side

      Speed and FRW on gear

      Each line is a setup’s running speed (its whirlwinding speed when it has Whirlwind) as its FRW on gear goes from 0 to 300%, its skills as they are; the dot, with the setup’s letter, is where it is now. The dashed gray line is a character running with no movement skills.

        How it’s worked out

        • FRW on gear (with Vigor’s +1% per base point: the Paladin has it whichever aura is on) adds up, then counts as 150 × FRW / (150 + FRW), rounded down: 30% counts 25, 60% counts 42, 100% counts 60, 300% counts 100.
        • Movement from skills (Increased Speed, Frenzy, Burst of Speed, Vigor’s aura, Blaze, Evade, Feral Rage, Hunger, Joust) and from a Stamina shrine (+35%) counts in full. Frenzy’s grows with its stacks (a fifth of it per stack, full at 5); Feral Rage’s is worked out from its charges, as the game does. Feral Rage is the werewolf’s and Hunger the werebear’s, so a Druid has one of them at a time.
        • Blaze gives +25% movement at level 1 and 1% more a level, up to 60%, while it lasts: 3 seconds at level 1 and 0.2 more a level, up to 10. Any class can have it from an item (Infernostride casts level 16 when you’re struck: +40% for 6 seconds). It’s one effect, so a Sorceress’s own Blaze and an item’s don’t add up: the page counts one at a time. While it lasts it leaves fire behind you as you move, and each flame burns for 2.4 seconds.
        • Charge sets its own speed when it starts: the run speed with Charge’s +150% added to the movement % from skills, shrines, and slows (at least 50%, so Decrepify or a chill slows a charge too). FRW and running’s +50% don’t count, so it’s 23.44 yards a second with nothing else. In the race the Paladin charges the whole way (the strike at the end left out; when a buff runs out it charges again). Its legs play a frame of the run each game frame whatever the speed, and the two fainter copies behind it stand for Charge’s trails.
        • Whirlwind moves you at walking speed with every movement % that counts while walking (FRW on gear as above, your skills, a shrine, slows) plus Whirlwind’s own: the “Velocity” its description shows, +2% for each level after the first, up to +65% from level 34. Running’s +50% doesn’t count, so FRW and your other bonuses raise your whirlwinding speed as they raise your walking speed. With 40% FRW and Whirlwind 20 (+38%) and nothing else, that’s 10.56 yards a second whirlwinding against 11.31 running. In the race the Barbarian whirls the whole way, in the attack pose Whirlwind holds him in, with its own effect around him.
        • Speed: walking is 6 points of speed, running 9 (walking + 50%), and every 1% of movement adds 0.06 of a point. The game moves you a sixteenth of a map square per point every frame, 25 frames a second; in the yards the skill descriptions use (a map square is two thirds of a yard), a point is 25/24 of a yard a second: walking 6.25, running 9.375, and every 1% adds 0.0625. Calculators that read the points as yards a second show about 4% less. The speed never goes below a quarter of walking.
        • The race uses the game’s own pictures, facing east (screen right), where a yard is 34 of the game’s pixels across. The animation keeps pace with the speed: running plays 101/256 of a frame per game frame and walking 213/256 (the werewolf 104 and 128, the werebear 120 and 168), times the same speed % that moves you, so a faster character also moves its legs faster. Each setup starts with its buffs just cast, and a buff that runs out on the way stops counting there: Joust’s lasts 2 seconds and Blaze’s 3 to 10, the others longer than any race here.
        • Slows take off the same movement % your skills add to, in full. Cold damage chills you for −50% while it lasts. A monster’s Decrepify, Holy Freeze, or Slow Movement takes what its skill level gives, and a monster’s skills are 3 levels higher in Nightmare and 7 in Hell. Duriel’s Holy Freeze is the exception: −42% in Normal and Nightmare and −44% in Hell. Map bosses, map events, and ubers are in Hell. An aura-enchanted unique monster has one of 8 auras, Holy Freeze among them, at its own level divided by 7 (at most 13): from −30% at monster level 1 to 13 up to −51% at level 91 and above, in every difficulty. In the race, chill and Holy Freeze turn the runner blue and Decrepify and Slow Movement show their effects, as the game draws them. Slows add up with each other and with your buffs: chilled with Frenzy’s +58%, you keep +8%. Armor and shields don’t slow you.
        • Not shown: Blade Dance’s own movement, Teleport, and what stops you for a moment (spider webs, a map boss’s stun).
