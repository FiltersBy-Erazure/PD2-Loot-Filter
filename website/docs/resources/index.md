# Resources

Reference pages on how parts of Project Diablo 2 work, to look things up while you play. More will be added over
time.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **[Infinity Merc Bases](infinity-merc-bases.md)**

    ---

    Which ethereal polearm or spear to put Infinity in for the Act 2 mercenary: damage, Crushing Blows and kill time at

-   **[Map Experience](map-experience.md)**

    ---

    What the filter's two experience lines on a map mean (`1.4x Experience`, `1.1x at Level 89`), how your level and

-   **[Event and Boss Map Runs](event-boss-runs.md)**

    Teleport over a rare map for its map event, do it, kill the map boss, next map: against full clears, an hour of

    Ear, experience, and which events pay their time. And what rushing a unique map's bosses is worth for the grail.

-   **[Ohm vs Jewel](ohm-vs-jewel.md)**

    ---

    Does a jewel beat an Ohm rune in your weapon? The minimum and maximum damage a jewel needs to match Ohm's enhanced
    damage, exact for your weapon and what is already on it, and how the jewels that can drop compare.

-   **[Damage Calculator](damage-calculator.md)**

    ---

    Up to four builds side by side: weapon, Strength and Dexterity, attack skill, auras, critical and deadly strike,
    crushing blow. Their damage per hit, frames per attack, damage per second and kill time, and what else would give
    the same damage.

-   **[Run Speed](run-speed.md)**

    ---

    How fast you run with your faster run/walk and your movement skills, up to four setups side by side, and what more
    FRW on gear would still bring.

-   **[IAS Calculator](ias-calculator.md)**

    ---

    Frames per attack for every class, werewolf and werebear, weapon and attack skill: the IAS breakpoints, a grid of
    weapon IAS against IAS on other gear, the fastest setups of each class, and every attack played frame by frame.
