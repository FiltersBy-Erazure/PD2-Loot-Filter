# Filter Levels

What each filter level shows, notifies and hides, for every kind of item that drops, read from the filter's own rules:
the table first, then what changes from one level to the next. Pick a filter level in the launcher.

Super Healing and Super Mana Potions show on every filter level. To hide healing or mana potions on any level, turn on
Hide Healing Potions or Hide Mana Potions in the game's Loot Filter options: they hide every healing or mana potion,
whatever the filter level.
