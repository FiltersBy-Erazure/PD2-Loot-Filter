    Crushing Blows per second

    Every hit rolls Crushing Blow, both hits of a Jab included, so this follows the hit rate alone: the weapon's damage doesn't matter. Infinity's Chain Lightning (25% on striking) fires at the same rate, scaled to 25%.

      Seconds to kill the target

      Weapon damage (Critical and Deadly Strikes included) and Crushing Blow together, the merc alone. Lower is better. The more each hit does, the more the harder-hitting bases gain on the faster ones, whose edge is the extra Crushing Blows.

        Teleporting past a monster

        You land on a monster and teleport on one cast later: 8 frames at 105% FCR, 7 frames at 200% FCR. Your merc is moved to where you land in the same frame; if it's free, it acts 2 frames later and Jabs the monster beside it. That Jab plays out in full, both hits on that monster, even after your next teleport has moved the merc on with you: the hit never checks distance. The chart adds up what that one attack does to the monster set above, frame by frame from the frame you land, at your IAS; the markers show when you teleport on.

          Item IAS needed for each Jab length, with your skill IAS. A Jab's length covers both of its hits; the merc stands one more frame before its next attack; 25 frames make one second.

          Basic attack breakpoints

          One hit per swing; the merc swings instead of Jabbing 20% of the time.

          How it's worked out

          • Attack speed. Item IAS counts as ⌊120 × IAS / (120 + IAS)⌋. Add skill IAS, subtract the base's speed (negative is faster), cap at 75: that's the total. The animation then advances ⌊256 × (100 + total) / 100⌋ per frame. The merc's Jab is a 14-step sequence with two hits (on steps 6 and 10), lasting ⌈256 × 14 / rate⌉ frames; its basic attack is a 16-frame swing with one hit (on frame 11), lasting ⌈256 × 16 / rate⌉ − 1. These give the PD2 wiki's Desert Guard breakpoints exactly. Between two attacks the merc stands one more frame before it decides on the next one.
          • Attack mix. The merc uses Jab 80% of the time and a basic attack otherwise, so damage and hits per second are averages over that mix (or Jab alone, if you pick it).
          • Damage per hit. First the weapon's own damage: the base's damage, times 1.25 for ethereal, raised by the weapon's enhanced damage, which is Infinity's roll plus a superior base's 5–20% (both are on the weapon, so they add up). That is the damage the weapon shows. A hit does the weapon's damage times (100 + the Strength and Dexterity bonus + Jab's bonus + other damage %) / 100: enhanced damage on the merc's other gear adds to those bonuses, not to the weapon's. Polearms and spears give 0.75% per point of Strength and of Dexterity. Jab gives +30% at level 1 and +15% per level; the merc's Jab is level 24 from merc level 75 on, plus your + skills. A basic attack gets no Jab bonus. A Hell merc has 173 Strength and 139 Dexterity at level 75, then 1.75 and 1.5 more per level.
          • Critical Strike and Deadly Strike. Every hit, both Jab hits included, first rolls Critical Strike (at most 75%): it does 200% damage, plus any Critical Strike Multiplier. A hit that isn't a Critical Strike rolls Deadly Strike (at most 75%, plus any Maximum Deadly Strike): 150% damage, plus any Deadly Strike Multiplier. So 50% Deadly Strike alone makes the average hit 25% stronger. Only the hit's physical damage is raised; Crushing Blow takes the same share as without. Every base gains the same share, so neither changes which base does the most damage per second.
          • Crushing Blow. Every hit rolls the chance, both Jab hits included. A proc takes the target's current life divided by 8 for most monsters (normal, champion, unique, minion), 30 for map bosses, or 70 + 10 × the % of life it's missing for prime evils. A merc's Crushing Blow works exactly like a player's. Efficiency divides the divisor by (1 + efficiency), a missile multiplies it by 1.5, physical resistance lowers the damage (none at 100%), and with more players in the game the divisor grows (with one player it doesn't).
          • Kill time. Each hit removes the average damage (Critical and Deadly Strikes averaged in) and the average Crushing Blow (chance × share × life left), until no life is left; the hit count divided by hits per second is the time. The merc alone, one player. Baal's life and physical resistance are his Hell values with one player (6,336 base life at 7,792% for his level 99); the map monster and map boss are round examples, so type your own target's.
          • Teleporting. The sorceress's cast is 14 frames at the same speed scale. FCR counts as ⌊120 × FCR / (120 + FCR)⌋, at most 75, and a cast lasts ⌈256 × 14 / rate⌉ − 1 frames: 8 at 105% FCR, 7 at 200%. You land on one cast's teleport frame and leave on the next one's, so you stand on the monster for exactly one cast.
          • The merc during a pass. When you teleport, your merc is moved to where you land in the same frame (except in guild games). If it was free, it acts 2 frames later; if it was in the middle of an attack, that attack keeps going and keeps its target. A Jab's hits land ⌈256 × 5 / rate⌉ and ⌈256 × 9 / rate⌉ frames after it starts, a basic attack's at ⌈256 × 11 / rate⌉, and nothing checks distance between the hit frame and the damage, so a Jab finishes on its first target wherever the merc has been moved. When you walk or run away instead, the merc heads back once you're 19 subtiles away and it has no target (about 34 while it fights), and warps beside you past 48.
          • Not counted. Walking to a target, misses, Chain Lightning's damage, and the merc's own base damage (whether it adds to the weapon's damage isn't confirmed).
