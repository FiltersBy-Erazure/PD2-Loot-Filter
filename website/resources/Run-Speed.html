      How it’s worked out

      • FRW on gear (with Vigor’s +1% per base point: the Paladin has it whichever aura is on) adds up, then counts as 150 × FRW / (150 + FRW), rounded down: 30% counts 25, 60% counts 42, 100% counts 60, 300% counts 100.
      • Movement from skills (Increased Speed, Frenzy, Burst of Speed, Vigor’s aura, Blaze, Evade, Feral Rage, Hunger, Joust) and from a Stamina shrine (+35%) counts in full. Frenzy’s grows with its stacks (a fifth of it per stack, full at 5); Feral Rage’s is worked out from its charges, as the game does. Feral Rage is the werewolf’s and Hunger the werebear’s, so a Druid has one of them at a time.
      • Blaze gives +25% movement at level 1 and 1% more a level, up to 60%, while it lasts: 3 seconds at level 1 and 0.2 more a level, up to 10. Any class can have it from an item (Infernostride casts level 16 when you’re struck: +40% for 6 seconds). While it lasts it leaves fire behind you as you move, and each flame burns for 2.4 seconds.
      • Charge sets its own speed when it starts: the run speed with Charge’s +150% added to the movement % from skills and shrines (at least 50%). FRW and running’s +50% don’t count, so it’s 23.44 yards a second with nothing else. In the race the Paladin charges the whole way (the strike at the end left out; when a buff runs out it charges again). Its legs play a frame of the run each game frame whatever the speed, and the two fainter copies behind it stand for Charge’s trails.
      • Speed: walking is 6 points of speed, running 9 (walking + 50%), and every 1% of movement adds 0.06 of a point. The game moves you a sixteenth of a map square per point every frame, 25 frames a second; in the yards the skill descriptions use (a map square is two thirds of a yard), a point is 25/24 of a yard a second: walking 6.25, running 9.375, and every 1% adds 0.0625. Calculators that read the points as yards a second show about 4% less. The speed never goes below a quarter of walking.
      • The race uses the game’s own pictures, facing east (screen right), where a yard is 34 of the game’s pixels across. The animation keeps pace with the speed: running plays 101/256 of a frame per game frame and walking 213/256 (the werewolf 104 and 128, the werebear 120 and 168), times the same speed % that moves you, so a faster character also moves its legs faster. Each setup starts with its buffs just cast, and a buff that runs out on the way stops counting there: Joust’s lasts 2 seconds and Blaze’s 3 to 10, the others longer than any race here.
      • Not shown: Whirlwind’s and Blade Dance’s own movement, and Teleport.
