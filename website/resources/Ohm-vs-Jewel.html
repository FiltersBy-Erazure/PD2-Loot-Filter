What a jewel needs to match Ohm

A jewel with the minimum and the maximum damage needed matches Ohm’s minimum and its maximum damage. “Same average” is what the jewel’s minimum and maximum damage must add up to for the same average hit: a jewel with only maximum damage needs that much maximum damage.

Jewels that can roll

Each jewel has one affix of each kind at most: enhanced damage, minimum or maximum damage as a prefix, minimum or maximum damage as a suffix. A magic jewel has one prefix and one suffix; a rare jewel four affixes, three of a side at most. Each line is its best rolls of the highest tiers, against Ohm in this weapon, at its lowest and its top rolls.

How it’s worked out

  • Enhanced damage on the weapon raises only its base damage. The weapon’s own enhanced damage, a superior base’s and every rune and jewel in its sockets add up, and the weapon’s damage is its base damage plus that percentage of it, rounded down. Its minimum and maximum damage (affixes, sockets) are added after, so enhanced damage never raises them.
  • An ethereal weapon has 5/4 of its base’s damage, rounded down, before anything else.
  • Ohm against a jewel: Ohm adds 45% of the base damage, a jewel its own enhanced damage of it plus its flat damage. A jewel needs the flat damage that makes up the difference, which the page works out with the rounding of the weapon you entered: one point either way decides it more often than not.
  • Everything else multiplies both alike. Strength or Dexterity, skills, auras and enhanced damage on other gear raise the weapon’s damage by the same percentage with Ohm or with the jewel, and flat damage from other gear (rings, charms) is the same either way, so the difference in the weapon’s damage decides. The page compares the weapon’s damage; your whole attack differs by a little less when other gear adds flat damage.
  • A hit rolls any damage from its minimum to its maximum alike, so the average hit is halfway between. When a jewel’s minimum damage lifts the minimum to the maximum or above, the maximum becomes the minimum + 1.
