The filter puts two experience lines on every identified map, under its other highlights. The first says how good the map's rolls are; the second, what the map is worth to your character right now. This page explains both, and shows how your level and a map's size change the second one.

1.4x Experience: how good the rolls are

A full clear of this map with its rolls, against the same map with no rolls: 1.4x means the rolls add 40%. Which map it is doesn't matter here, only what its rolls add to it, and your level doesn't change it.

  • What counts: Monster Density (more monsters), Monster Rarity (more champion and unique packs), Experience, Area Level (higher level monsters, each worth more), Fortified (half as many monsters, each worth double) and "Map contains ..." monsters (one more kind of monster in the mix).
  • What doesn't: anything that makes monsters easier or harder to kill, and the loot rolls.

Its color

The color says how the rolls compare with other maps of the same tier. Every tier is cut the same way among rare maps: about 1 in 10 shows no line, half are gold, a third red and 1 in 20 purple.

Unique maps use the colors of the tier their area level matches: 87 T1, 88 T2, 89 T3. T4 dungeons are cut among their rare maps, which come from the Horadric Cube (a T3 map and a Dungeon Scarab) with +40–50% experience besides their rolls.

1.1x at Level 89: what the map is worth to you

This map with its rolls: the experience of a full clear, for you, divided by the distance you walk to clear it
The average non-magical T1–T3 map: the same, for you

1.0x is an average plain map. 1.1x is 10% more experience for the same distance walked. Below 1.0x, an average plain map would do better. T4 dungeons are compared with the average plain T3 map instead.

  • The map itself: how many monsters spawn, how many champion and unique packs, their level, and the bosses in its layout.
  • Its rolls: the same ones as in the Experience line.
  • The distance: every map has a clear path, the route that takes you close to 95% of the places its monsters spawn (most players clear 90–95% of a map), averaged over its layouts and starting points. A big, spread-out map takes longer to clear, so its experience counts for less.
  • Your level: monsters well below your level give less experience. More on that below.
  • Your own +% experience: the map's Experience roll adds to the bonus you already have; it doesn't multiply it. So it's worth less the more you have: with +50% of your own, a map's +15% gives you 10% more, not 15%.

A monster up to 5 levels below you gives full experience, and each level more takes 19% off, down to 5%. A monster above your level gives your level divided by its level.

    What it leaves out

    • How fast you kill: monster life, damage, resistances and immunities, and your build. It assumes the time a clear takes grows with the distance you walk.
    • Loot: Magic Find and the drop rolls.
    • Random map events.
    • Everything between maps: time in town, loading screens, opening maps.

    Good to know

    • Both lines show on identified maps only: an unidentified map has no rolls yet.
    • The "at Level" line shows for characters of level 80–98. At level 99 there's no experience to gain, so there's no line.
    • Both numbers are cut to one decimal, not rounded: 1.19 shows as 1.1x, and 0.99 as 0.9x.
    • They are estimates, worked out from how maps are built and how monsters spawn, not counted in game.

    How it's worked out

    • Monsters. Every area of a map spawns monsters in its rooms. Monster Density adds spawns, up to a limit each area has. Champion and unique packs fall between the area's own minimum and maximum, and Monster Rarity raises both. Fortified halves the spawns and doubles each monster's experience. A "Map contains ..." monster joins the area's own monsters as one more kind, picked for about 1 spawn in 10 (1 in 7 to 1 in 20, depending on the area). The bosses and monsters placed in a map's layout count too.
    • Experience per monster. It follows the monster's level: the map's area level, plus any Area Level roll (each level is worth about 2.5% more around level 87), 2 more for champions and 3 more for uniques and their minions.
    • Your level. Each kind of monster gets the level penalty above: 81%, 62%, 43%, 24% and then 5% for a monster 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 or more levels below you. Then your own +% experience and the map's are added together and applied.
    • Clear path. Measured on the map's own layouts: from where you start, walk to the nearest place a monster can spawn that you haven't come close to yet, until you've come close to 95% of them. Averaged over every layout of the map and five starting points. Horazon's Memory's lightning spires are left out (players walk past them).
    • The 1.0x. The average of the 29 T1–T3 maps with no rolls: their average experience for you over their average clear path. For T4 dungeons, the average of the T3 maps.
