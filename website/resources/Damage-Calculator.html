    How it’s worked out

    • The weapon: an ethereal weapon has 5/4 of its base damage, rounded down. Enhanced damage on the weapon (its own, a superior base’s, its sockets’) raises only the base damage: base + base × enhanced damage / 100, rounded down; then its flat minimum and maximum damage are added.
    • The attack: the weapon’s damage plus flat damage from other gear, times (100 + the attack skill’s damage + your auras’ and buffs’ damage + Strength × the weapon’s Strength bonus / 100 + Dexterity × its Dexterity bonus / 100 + your weapon mastery + enhanced damage from other gear) / 100. A minimum below 1 becomes 1 and a maximum at or below the minimum becomes the minimum + 1, before the percentages. The game keeps it in 256ths of a point; a hit is any damage from the minimum to the maximum alike. A skill that deals part of the weapon’s damage (Stun, Guided Arrow, Blade Fury) takes that part.
    • Your own aura gives you its full damage bonus; a party member’s or your mercenary’s gives half (the “Party Damage” of the tooltip).
    • Critical strike comes first: chance (Amazon’s Critical Strike, items, a mastery) at most 75%, double damage plus the critical strike multiplier. Only a hit that isn’t critical can be a deadly strike: chance at most 75% plus maximum deadly strike, 1.5 times damage plus the deadly strike multiplier.
    • Physical resistance: the monster’s resistance plus Amplify Damage or Decrepify (one curse at a time) and Battle Cry. Against a monster whose own resistance is 100% or more, curses and Battle Cry count half. Physical pierce lowers what is left below 100%, and below 0% it counts half. Damage is lowered by the resistance (raised at negative resistance, at most double).
    • Crushing blow takes a share of the life the monster has left, whatever its life: 1/8 for a monster (champions and uniques too), 1/30 for a map boss, 1/10 for a player or a pet, and 1/70 for another boss at full life, less as it loses life (1/(70 + 10 for every 1% it is missing)). Each player after the first adds 1.4 to the 8, 30 or 70 (their 70% more life, / 50). A ranged attack divides by 1.5 times as much; crushing blow efficiency multiplies the share by (1 + efficiency / 100). The monster’s physical resistance lowers it (none at 100% or more; pierce doesn’t count). More chance and more efficiency are always better; the kill time shows how much, beside the time without it.
    • Frames per attack come from the IAS Calculator, which has the whole model and plays each attack frame by frame: speed = 100 + the skills’ speed (Fanaticism, Frenzy by its charges, Burst of Speed, Werewolf, the attack’s own) − the weapon’s speed modifier + effective IAS (120 × IAS / (120 + IAS)), 30 less for a sequence, kept between 15 and 175; an animation of N frames takes N × 256 / (its speed × speed / 100) frames, rounded up, minus 1, held. A normal attack is attack 1 or attack 2, half the time each, so it counts both, and it hits with the first weapon only. Two weapons: only the faster one’s speed counts. Zeal, Fend, Fury, Strafe and Dragon Talon rewind their animation between hits (Strafe: its fewest arrows, at one monster); Whirlwind and Blade Dance hit every 8 frames (unslowed), with each weapon, whatever the attack speed; Charge’s strike runs at a speed the game sets when it connects. A druid’s wereform attacks run at a speed set by the weapon’s attack speed in human form.
    • Next breakpoint: the increased attack speed a build still needs on its other gear (its weapon’s IAS as set) for fewer frames, and what that is worth in damage per second. A build at the most its attack counts shows none.
    • The bosses have their life and physical resistance in Hell: the life of the boss’s own level, +70% for each player after the first (at most 8,388,607). Diablo Clone, Rathma, Mendeln and Lucion have 1.5 times that at tier 0, 4.8 times at tier 1 and 5.6 times at tier 2; their phases, immunity, healing and helpers aren’t counted. The map and dungeon bosses on the game’s map boss lists take crushing blow as map bosses (1/30 of their life); The Tainted Hive, Na-Krul, the two Djinns and Zoltun Kulle aren’t on them and take it as other bosses do. Map modifiers aren’t counted.
    • How they compare: damage per second is the average hit × what critical and deadly strikes add × what the monster’s resistance lets through × hits per second, so a build’s difference from build A splits exactly into those four. Fields that differ from build A are marked gold.
    • Not counted: elemental, poison and magic damage, open wounds, life leech, the monster’s defense (chance to hit) and its blocking, splash damage to other monsters, mercenaries and summons.
