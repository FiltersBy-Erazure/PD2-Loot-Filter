Switch a variable roll on to show it as a tag (changing an item marks it reviewed). The black box is the label as the filter's own rules draw it (every filter level of the Main version, the item's highest rolls), cut where BH cuts long names (from the right end of the top line), for the base, ethereal state, sockets and corruption picked under it; it starts on the case with the longest label. The meter gives the longest label your picks make over all of them: every base the cube upgrades the item to, ethereal or not, no sockets or its most, and every corruption outcome its slot can get (amulets also desecrated), after the item's cut steps (tools/unique_cut_rules.txt). Beside each roll: its width at its highest roll and the longest label with it switched on (red: BH cuts it somewhere). A dashed switch is a roll with no tag line in the filter yet: picking it asks for one, and its tag shows dotted at the left end, in the look of a similar tag. Your clicks are kept in this browser until you press Save picks file; save it over tools/unique_roll_picks.txt, then double-click build.bat.
