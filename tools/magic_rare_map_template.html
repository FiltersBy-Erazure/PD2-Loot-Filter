__TITLE__

How to read it

How the Erazure loot filter shows __QUALITY__ items that drop on the ground, unidentified, read from the filter's own rules. At the filter levels a base's rows don't cover, it shows with its plain name in __COLOR__, with Eth before it when it is ethereal.

Notify minimap icon and chat line Shows as a look of its own (shown as the game draws it) Hidden the item is not shown
  • Each row is one case: the filter levels it applies to, what happens, and what the item needs. Every column must hold; any means no condition. Pick your filter level in the bar to see only your rows.
  • Crafted affix level is the affix level the filter works out for a craft made from the item: from half your character level plus half its item level.
  • Bases of one type and tier with the same socket maximum and rows share a group. A look shows the group's first base; each base shows its own name.
  • Hidden items still show in town. The minimap icon and chat line come on their own, even for a hidden item.
