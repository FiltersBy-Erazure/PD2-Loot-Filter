Erazure Potion Editor

How every potion shows on each filter level, at every character level, as the rules in draw it today, beside your choice. Paint the Your choice columns and edit the looks: your choices are saved here, and Claude writes them into the filter when you say so.

  • Paint: pick a look in a card's Paint with row, then click a cell, drag down a column, or shift-click to fill from the last cell you painted. A column's heading paints the whole column.
  • Character levels: type where a column starts (15, 50) to add or remove columns.
  • Looks: Edit on a look changes its text, minimap icon and chat line everywhere the card uses it; New look adds one. A small square in a cell is the minimap icon, a gold line under it the chat line.
  • Parts: each grid is one case, on the ground outside town in Nightmare and Hell first. A card shows a part for each case today's rules treat otherwise, and can split off Normal difficulty or maps.
  • A potion hidden on the ground keeps a look in town (pick which) and in shops. On level 0 every potion shows, and a minimap icon brings its chat line.
