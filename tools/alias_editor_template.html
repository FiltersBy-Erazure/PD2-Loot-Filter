Every alias that draws something, as Erazure - Main and Erazure - Minimal draw it. Edit either text and the labels redraw as you type; your edits are saved here so Claude can write them into sections/040-aliases.filter (Main) and styles/minimal.filter (Minimal). A Main change reaches Main and its seven other versions, Minimal wherever it draws Main's look, and Corrlorized wherever it keeps Main's look: each card says.
