Each row is a weapon type and each column a highlight tier of 320 (the stars around +412% Enhanced Damage), plus the ED tag's purple band from 300. Type a tier's lowest ED in a cell, or clear it to switch the tier off for that type. Under each number: how many items of that type reach it, for the item level, map and corruption picked below. Open a row for its chart. Your thresholds are saved as you type; the filter changes only when you tell Claude to apply them. Magic weapons keep today's thresholds.
