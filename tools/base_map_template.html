Erazure Non-magical Bases

How to read it

How the Erazure loot filter shows non-magical armor and weapons that drop on the ground, read from the filter's own rules. Anything a base's rows don't cover is shown as usual.

Tag a word before the name (shown as the game draws it) Notify minimap icon and chat line Shows as a display of its own Hidden the item is not shown
  • Each row is one case: the filter levels it applies to, what happens, and what the item needs. Every column must hold; any means no condition. Pick your filter level in the bar to see only your rows.
  • ED is enhanced defense on armor and enhanced damage on weapons (superior items). Class items roll +1–3 to skills; a runeword base with several skill combinations opens to list them.
  • Bases of one type and tier with the same socket maximum, size and rows share a group.
  • Hidden items still show in town. The minimap icon and chat line come on their own, even for a hidden item. Low quality items (Cracked, Crude, Damaged) have their own rows when they differ.
