    What's New¶

    What changed in each update of the filter, newest first. Your Horadric Cube's description shows the update you have, such as Season 14 - November 16th - v2, and the launcher brings each new one the next time you press Play. Since November 4th, a day with several updates numbers them: v1, v2, and so on. What's being worked on next is on Coming Up.

    November 26th¶

    Season 14 - November 26th - v1. In the filter, Low Level Duel uniques and set items get a teal Low Level Dueling line, more items join them, the LLD and Rathma tags add resistances together, rare maps get a plain gold density line, rare amulets of item level 90 and up get their high value look on filter level 12, and the filter loads faster. The website gets a new Enhanced Damage Odds page, a rebuilt LLD Item Guide that also counts the kills a drop takes, spells and elemental damage in the Damage Calculator, and loading your character from the PD2 armory.

    In the filter

    • Low Level Duel uniques and set items show a teal Low Level Dueling line under their name on every filter level: unidentified, and once identified while uncorrupted or after a corruption that suits a duel on that kind of item (on weapons, the physical ones). Their Low Level Duel look, with the teal stars, now shows on filter levels 0-7 (set items 0-4) only for characters of level 80 or more; below that they look like the other uniques and sets of their value. Levels 8-11 keep the [LLD] tag.
    • More Low Level Duel items: The Spirit Shroud, Ripsaw, Steeldriver, Rakescar, Twitchthroe, Venom Ward, Heavenly Garb, Biggin's Bonnet, and Hawkmail, and the set items Death's Guard, Death's Hand, Natalya's Soul, and Whitstan's Guard. Steelclash and Tancred's Hobnails keep their value look and get the line; Angelic Halo and Angelic Wings get it once identified.
    • LLD tag on rings, amulets, boots, and gloves: fire, cold, lightning, and poison resistance now add up, a point at 20, 40, 60, and 80 in all, instead of points for each resistance roll. Rare boots get the tag about twice as often, rings half again as often, amulets 2.5 times as often, and gloves a fifth more.
    • Rathma tag: fire, cold, and poison resistance add up, with cuts per kind of item: rings 20, 40, and 60; amulets and shields 30, 60, and 90; belts and gloves 15, 30, and 45; body armor 60 and 90; circlets 30 and 60. Shields get it more than twice as often, rings and circlets about a fifth more.
    • Jewel highlights: a rare jewel with 30%+ enhanced damage shows Physical with two of −15% requirements, +5 strength, +5 dexterity, +50 attack rating, and 7% FHR, and now also of 20%+ to one resistance, 5%+ all resistances, 15%+ magic find, 25%+ gold find, minimum and maximum damage adding up to 10+, and 3 life or mana after each kill. A magic jewel with all resistances 10+ shows All Resist with one of 7% FHR, +15 life, −15% requirements, +6 strength or dexterity, 15% IAS, minimum and maximum damage adding up to 10+, or any magic find. A magic jewel with a highlight no longer says "Cube Jewels to create Jewel Fragments".
    • Rare amulets on filter level 12: item level 90 and up get the high value look, minimap icon, drop sound, and chat notification, as on the other levels; under 90 they keep the low value look with a small icon and no chat notification.
    • Map density and rarity: a rare map whose density is just under the gold cut (T1 56-67%, T2 60-73%, T3 65-79%, corrupted ones too) shows +X% Monster Density in gold without stars, when its rarity is under gold. The red and purple Monster Rarity lines get the same bigger star frames as the density lines. Descent from Mount Arreat maps show their +X% Fire Damage line, as the other maps whose monsters deal fire do. Map Lines shows both.
    • Quivers: corrupted and crafted quivers tag the stats their corruption or craft adds (magic find, gold find, life, a resistance, attack rating, life and mana stolen, mana after each kill) at any item level, and get FHR and FCR tags.
    • Labels: 25 unique and set labels that were cut short on the bases the cube upgrades them to (ethereal, socketed, or corrupted too), such as The Jade Tan Do, Soulflay, Buriza-Do Kyanon, and Grim's Burning Dead, now fit. Gloom's roll tag shows its Curse Resistance roll instead of FHR, which is always 20% in Season 14.
    • Small fixes: magic and non-magical scepters with +3 to Combat Skills get their highlight again (below character level 60, Paladins below 80). Ethereal War Clubs and Martels de Fer with 4 or 6 sockets get the minimap icon and chat notification too (filter levels 0-2, below character level 80). A runeword in a non-ethereal javelin or throwing weapon shows its Sell value and Cost lines in gold, as every other runeword does.
    • Faster loading: the game has about 7% fewer rules to read when it loads the filter (5,979 instead of 6,410 in Erazure - Main), with every look the same.

    On the website

    • Enhanced Damage Odds is a new Resources page: how often a rare or crafted weapon of each type rolls at least 250%, 300%, 400%, 500%, 600%, or 700% enhanced damage, where it drops or how you craft it, with a chart per weapon type and the stars the filter draws at each.
    • LLD Item Guide is rebuilt around the item level to aim for. It ranks every way to make an item by its chance per item (a drop, 3 perfect gems, 3 Perfect Skulls, bricking with a Worldstone Shard, crafting, and buying from a vendor) and says where monsters of that level are, the character level and item to craft with, and about how many kills a drop takes at your magic find. Pick an item Erazure tags LLD and its affixes are filled in, plan a corruption on top, and read How to use this guide at the top.
    • Item Builder: bases by kind and tier (Amazon Bows, Druid Pelts, Polearms · Exceptional), one search for affixes on both sides, each affix's details on two lines, and craft names in their gem's color.
    • Damage Calculator counts elemental damage and spells: the Sorceress's, Necromancer's, Druid's, and Assassin's spells and traps, the Paladin's holy spells, and elemental attacks such as Fists of Fire, Rabies, and Lightning Fury, many with a ready-made build to start from. Compare two sets of gear, see your chance to kill a monster within 1-6 hits, and undo or redo a change.
    • Your character from the PD2 armory: type your character's name to load its gear and stats into the Damage Calculator, the IAS Calculator, Breakpoints, and the Monster Damage Calculator.
    • IAS Calculator: in werewolf and werebear form, Feral Rage, Fire Claws, and Fury speed up partway through an attack, as in the game, so these attacks can take a frame less than the calculator showed (a player's report); a Connection setting (single player or online) times it, and a cooldown's wait includes your ping online. The page also starts from your own setup in fewer steps, takes a weapon pasted from the game, shows every number in its tables, and has a shorter Fastest Frames and a Reference with contents.
    • Grail Planner, Rune Odds, and Map Event and Boss Rushing count the Bitter Depths' monsters about 8% lower; their numbers barely move.

    November 23rd¶

    Season 14 - November 23rd - v1. In the filter, jewels worth a second look get a highlight; rings get a new Caster highlight and a stricter Physical one; rare and crafted rings, amulets, boots, and gloves can get the LLD tag; and the Rathma, D.Clone, and Lucion tags add up an item's strength, dexterity, and vitality. The website gets eleven new pages, such as the Item Lookup, a Tags and Symbols page, an Icons and Sounds page, and a Shadow of Mendeln page, and improves many others.

    In the filter

    • Jewel highlights: identified magic and rare jewels worth a second look get a green star line under their name, as charms do. The line says what the jewel is good for: Physical, 5% Fire Damage (or Light, Cold, Poison, or Magic), All Resist, or Caster, and a jewel gets one at most. The cut-offs come from the asking prices of about 8,000 jewels listed on the ladder trade site; the full rules are under this list.
    • LLD on jewels is stricter: a lone +12-15 maximum damage roll no longer counts twice, and on magic jewels, single resistances, attack rating, open wounds, and elemental damage no longer count at all. About a third as many magic jewels get the tag as before.
    • Rings: rare and crafted rings get a new Caster highlight when they have 20% FCR and two more useful rolls (about 1 in 3 rings with 20% FCR do). The Physical highlight is stricter: about 1 in 200 rolled rare rings get it, where before 15% all resistances alone was enough. Skill & Shop Highlights lists what each one needs.
    • LLD on gear: rare and crafted rings, amulets, boots, and gloves that need level 30 or less get the teal LLD tag, at the end of their tag line, and a Low Level Dueling line when their stats add up to enough points. The LLD Item Guide shows which stats count on each kind of item and the best way to make one.
    • LLD on charms: attack rating now counts on every charm, and FCR and IAS count on Large Charms. Small Charms no longer count cold damage.
    • Rathma, D.Clone, and Lucion tags add strength, dexterity, and vitality together, and +5 to all attributes counts as 15. So a helm with +14 strength and +14 dexterity now counts toward a tag, as +15 to a single attribute did.
    • Highlights of one skill (like +6 Bone Spear) now sit under an item's Damage Reduction line and above its Max Resistances line.
    • Runewords you drop outside town are no longer hidden when their base is an Amazon bow, spear, or javelin, or a quiver. Runewords in a Holy Water Sprinkler show their name in gold.
    • Additional Boss: this map line is now red on Shadows of Westmarch, Pandemonium Citadel, Kyovashad, and Royal Crypts too, 12 maps in all. On the other T1–T3 maps it stays teal.
    The jewel rules in full

    A jewel shows one highlight at most: when it has what two of them need, the first in this order wins, so a rare jewel with 40% enhanced damage, +9 maximum damage, and +5% fire skill damage shows Physical. The LLD tag stays a line of its own, so a Low Level Dueling jewel can show both.

    • Magic jewels get a highlight with:
      • Physical: 30%+ enhanced damage with +15% IAS (a Ruby or Realgar Jewel of Fervor); enhanced damage plus minimum and maximum damage adding up to 48+ (38% enhanced damage with +10 minimum damage); or minimum and maximum damage adding up to 25+ (+13 and +12 maximum damage).
      • 5% Fire, Light, Cold, or Poison Damage: +5% to that element's skill damage with +15% IAS or −15% requirements. 5% Magic Damage also takes 7% FHR. +2-4% doesn't count.
      • All Resist: all resistances 14-15 with +15% IAS or −15% requirements, or 11+ with 7% FHR, +15 life, −15% requirements, +7 strength, or +7 dexterity. A Shimmering Jewel (5-10) never gets it.
    • Rare jewels get a highlight with:
      • Physical: enhanced damage plus minimum and maximum damage adding up to 45+, the point where a jewel adds more damage than an Ohm on a weapon of 100 damage (Ohm vs Jewel Comparison); minimum and maximum damage adding up to 25+; 30%+ enhanced damage with two of −15% requirements, +5 strength, +5 dexterity, +50 attack rating, and 7% FHR; enhanced damage plus minimum and maximum damage adding up to 34+ with open wounds or damage to demons or undead, any roll; or 80+ open wounds damage with 30+.
      • 5% Fire, Light, Cold, or Poison Damage: +5% to that skill damage with −15% requirements, life after each kill, or mana after each kill, or with two of 7% FHR, +7 strength, +7 dexterity, +7 energy, 25%+ to a single resistance, and +20 mana.
      • 5% Magic Damage: +5% magic skill damage, whatever else the jewel has.
      • All Resist: all resistances 9-10 with two of 7% FHR, +7 strength, +7 dexterity, +7 energy, 25%+ to a single resistance, life or mana after each kill, −15% requirements, 10%+ magic find, and 20%+ gold find.
      • Caster: life and mana after each kill, one of them +3.
    • Rare jewels, for example:
      • 33% enhanced damage, +13 maximum damage, 22% poison resistance, +6 life: Physical (46).
      • +12 minimum damage, +14 maximum damage, 25% poison resistance, 8% magic find: Physical (26 minimum and maximum damage).
      • 31% enhanced damage, +75 attack rating, −15% requirements, +6 life: Physical.
      • 34% enhanced damage, 33% damage to demons, +5 dexterity, 20% gold find: Physical. With 30% enhanced damage: no line.
      • 28% enhanced damage, 40% damage to undead, +8 maximum damage, +6 life: Physical (36).
      • 20% enhanced damage, open wounds (+35 damage), +15 maximum damage, +6 life: Physical (35).
      • Open wounds (+82 damage), 22% enhanced damage, +8 maximum damage, 20% gold find: Physical (30).
      • +5% fire skill damage, −15% requirements, 20% poison resistance, +6 life: 5% Fire Damage. With +4%: no line.
      • +5% lightning skill damage, +3 mana after each kill, 20% gold find, +6 life: 5% Light Damage.
      • 10% all resistances, 7% FHR, +6 life, 20% gold find: All Resist. With 8% magic find instead: no line.
      • +3 life after each kill, +2 mana after each kill, +6 life, 20% gold find: Caster.
      • +5% cold skill damage, 7% FHR, +8 energy, +6 life: 5% Cold Damage.
      • +5% magic skill damage, +9 maximum damage, 33% damage to demons, 6% magic find: 5% Magic Damage.

    On the website

    • Item Lookup is a new page: pick any item, or paste one you copied in game with Ctrl+C, to see how Erazure shows it on every filter level for your version and character. It also says in plain words what decides the look ("the rule for Sur, Ber, Jah, Cham, and Zod runes", or a unique's tier). Search the site for an item and the Item Lookup result opens right on it; each search result now shows just the item it found, such as Shako (uap), instead of a long list.
    • Tags and Symbols is a new page: what each tag in front of an item's name means (#fcr, -#%f, #r, 3fo, and more), shown in each color it comes in, the tags you'll see most often first, and the other marks around a name.
    • Icons and Sounds is a new page: every minimap icon the filter draws, grouped under headings (Runes, Gems and skulls, PD2 items, Maps, and more), each with its drop sound, its chat notification, and the items that get it.
    • Troubleshooting is a new page: what to check when the filter doesn't do what you expect. For an item that shows or hides when you didn't expect it, it starts with the Item Lookup.
    • Filter Levels: Which level for me?, at the top of the page, asks four questions and suggests a filter level and a version. When something you collect changes the level, it tells you why first. You can also compare any two filter levels, item by item.
    • Unique & Set Tiers: a new Once identified section under each item shows its label at its lowest and highest rolls, and with each corruption the Cube can give it, with every tag explained. These looks are the same in every Erazure and Minimal version.
    • Non-magical Bases: Bases for a runeword shows only the bases a runeword fits, and the finished runeword's label. Type a runeword's name in the search to get there.
    • Item pages: Unique & Set Tiers, Non-magical Bases, Magic Items, and Rare Items keep your filter level and search in the page's address, so a link opens the page just as you left it. Each row has a ⋯ menu to copy a link to it, open it in the Item Lookup or the Grail Planner, change it in the Filter Editor, or report a problem with it. At 200% zoom their bars scroll away instead of covering half the window, and on phones their buttons are easier to tap.
    • Examples and Versions: tap or click a label in a picture to see what the item is and which filter levels show it, and to hear its drop sound in each version.
    • Minimal Version has a search, and a play button beside each look that has a drop sound. Skill & Shop Highlights can show only your class's highlights.
    • Your character, once: save your class, level, and stats on one page, and the calculators, the map pages, the Grail Planner, and the Filter Editor's map lines all use them.
    • Calculators: each one has Reset to the defaults and Copy a link to this setup in the same place. You can send your setup from one calculator to another without losing what you had there: IAS to Damage, Damage to IAS, Ohm vs Jewel to IAS, and an Act 2 mercenary's IAS to Infinity Base Comparison. The Damage Calculator compares weapons on one build and works out Dragon Talon's kicks, and you can paste a weapon you copied in game with Ctrl+C to fill in its stats. On phones, its monster settings fold into one line.
    • Breakpoints is a new Resources page: your cast rate, hit recovery, and block frames for every class, form, and weapon, and what it takes to reach the next breakpoint.
    • Item Builder is a new Resources page: pick the affixes you want to see how likely an item with them is, and where it drops best. The new LLD Item Guide does the same for Low Level Dueling items, and its answer spells out the item it's for. It starts from what Erazure tags LLD: pick a charm, ring, amulet, pair of boots, or pair of gloves the filter tags, and it ranks every way to make one.
    • Corruption Outcomes is a new Resources page: what corrupting an item or a map can do, with the chance of each outcome.
    • Map Lines is a new Resources page: every line the filter writes under a map's name, the experience lines included, with its colors and how often it shows, worked out for your character where a line depends on it. The density and rarity star lines are drawn as they look in game.
    • Rune Odds is a new Resources page: how often each rune drops when you play solo, and where to farm it. In a T3 corrupted rare map, for example, a Vex or higher rune drops about once every 43,500 kills.
    • Shadow of Mendeln is a new Resources page: how much experience the map event's 45 waves give at your level, and the four choices that give more: Fortified, a party of 8, a T3 map, and +1 area level.
    • Map pages: Rune Odds, Corruption Outcomes, Map Lines, and Shadow of Mendeln have Reset to the defaults and Copy a link to this setup too, and no map page's bar takes up a third of a phone's screen anymore. Map Event and Boss Rushing now counts the Shadow of Mendeln's experience and how monsters really spawn, and its tables fit a laptop's window again. It values the events at this season's prices: the Catalyst Shards from the Altar now count (about 67 WSS an event), and the Spire shows what its keys are worth.
    • Grail Planner: share your list as a link or a picture from the new row of buttons under your plan. Your pace comes from when you mark items discovered, and filling in a grail you already had no longer dates all of it today. The grail curve shows where 8 in 10 players reach 90% of the grail. It now counts down the items you're still missing, so the last ones get as much room as the first, and marks how many maps it takes to find half, 90%, 95%, and 99% of the grail. The planner now starts using your Corrupted Ears at map 50, when most players are geared enough to run eared maps (you can pick 1, 100, 250, or 500 instead), and adds a line for using only the ears your own maps drop, about one ear for every 5 rare maps. Non-magical maps no longer count any ears, since an ear goes only on a magic or rare map.
    • Map Experience: see how many full clears it takes to reach your next level, for each tier's average non-magical map and for a rolled rare map (its own Experience roll counted). A new T4 dungeons section explains why a dungeon's "at Level" line reads 8x and higher, shows each dungeon at your level (80 to 98) as it comes from the Cube and with one corruption, and how many dungeons take you to your next level, and works through a Steppes of Daken-Shar. Dungeons are dangerous and slow: the line measures experience against the distance you walk, not the time it takes.
    • Filter Editor: Quick setup can give you fewer notifications, and you can put a ready-made set (Color-blind friendly or Self-found) on top of your version: add it to your changes, start a new profile with it, or let it replace your changes. The editor's bar has a search: type an item (Ber, Shako) to start a change for it, or a word (sound, color, icon, save) to jump to the right place.
    • Updates: a bar at the top of every page names the newest update until you close it, What's New gets a dot when there's an update you haven't seen, and the site tells you when a custom filter you saved in the Filter Editor is from an older update, so you can save it again for the latest changes. The row of pages under the tabs is now grouped: Loot Filter's pages under Get started and Look up, and Resources' under Your character, Gear, and Maps.
    • Words explained: the first time a section uses a word like affix level, item level, ethereal, Treacherous, IAS, FCR, FHR, FRW, PDR, or MF, hover over it or tap it to see what it means.
    • Easier to read: small gray text on the calculators and map pages has more contrast, links in text are underlined, and lists have a comma before their last "and" or "or".

    November 21st¶

    Season 14 - November 21st - v1, v2. Diadems and Tiaras worth bricking show the brick look, the experience numbers on maps are recounted, and the website gets a Feedback tab.

    • Diadems and Tiaras (v1): unsocketed non-magical ones of item level 85 and up show the brick look on filter levels 5 and up, the name with a red star and the note "You can Corrupt this for a 50% Chance to Brick into a Rare", instead of [Imbue] and "This can be Imbued into a Rare". On level 12 they are still hidden on the ground from character level 90. They no longer get a minimap dot or chat notification on those levels. Levels 0-4 keep [Imbue].
    • Map experience (v2): both experience lines on a map, the tag (1.72x Experience) and at Level, are recounted with how monsters really spawn. Monsters never spawn near where you arrive, big monsters need more room, and a map only spawns monsters in the parts around where you walk, while its chance of an elite pack counts its whole layout, empty space included. Maps laid out in a lot of empty space get fewer elite packs than we counted: at character level 90 a plain Lost Temple goes from 1.26x to 1.11x, Tomb of Zoltun Kulle from 1.02x to 0.96x, River of Blood from 0.98x to 0.93x, and Zhar's Sanctum from 1.14x to 0.84x. Kehjistan Marketplace rises from 0.78x to 0.95x (it is smaller to clear than we counted), Halls of Torture from 1.19x to 1.22x. Most maps move by 0.03x or less.
    • The experience tag's colors keep their meaning: gold from 1.49x, 1.52x, or 1.61x on T1, T2, or T3, red from 1.71x, 1.73x, or 1.81x, purple from 1.87x, 1.89x, or 2.00x; every uncorrupted dungeon shows it, red from 2.78x and purple from 3.04x. Without the new cuts, many more maps would have turned red or purple. The Map Experience page and the Grail Planner use the new numbers.
    • Feedback is a new tab: tell me when an item shows, hides, looks, or sounds wrong, suggest a change to the filter or to a page of this site, or say what doesn't work on a page. A few short questions, sent as an anonymous Google Form submission, with an option to add your Discord name if you'd like a reply. Questions are still quickest on Discord: erazure.
    • A Feedback button on every page (the speech bubble at the top, and "Feedback on this page" at the bottom) opens it with what that page knows already filled in: the calculator setup you were looking at, the filter level you picked on the item pages, or the Filter Editor's item and your changes.
    • Feedback on these changes: "Give feedback on changes" at the top of this page lets you tick the changes you want to comment on, then send a comment on each.

    November 20th¶

    Season 14 - November 20th - v1 to v2. Medium value set items you haven't discovered show the Grail look, maps get a Monster Defense warning, the last map warnings come in a new order, and the IAS Calculator gets a new layout and counts skill cooldowns.

    • Medium value set items you haven't discovered get a Grail look of their own: the Grail look's four stars a side, the outermost orange, with the white dots by the name that mark Medium value, and the set Grail icon. Until now they showed their Medium value look whether you had found them or not, so a new Tancred's Hobnails looked like any other pair. Since v2 they also play the Grail drop sound instead of their own. The Minimal and Corrlorized versions draw the Grail stars with Medium value's white arrows. High value set items, and High and Medium value uniques, still look the same either way. The Unique & Set Tiers page and the Grail Planner say so.
    • +X% Monster Defense is a new map warning for characters who attack: it says how much of your chance to hit the map's monsters take away. Red when you lose 10 points or more (95% down to 85%, say), purple from 20. It goes by your level, dexterity, attack rating, and +% attack rating on gear, with the attack skill bonus a typical build of your class has (the filter can't see your skill or weapon mastery), and by each map's own monsters: 14 maps' monsters are noticeably easier or harder to hit than most. Casters don't see it: a character with more faster cast rate than increased attack speed on gear (an Amazon, Assassin, or Barbarian may have up to 30 more), or with a low attack rating, or with Ignore Target's Defense. T1-T3 and unique maps, corrupted too, not Treacherous maps. The suffixes that bring it (+50% to +200% on about 1 rare map in 7, up to +300% on magic ones) also bring experience and magic find.
    • Map warnings: the lines between All Resistances and the density and rarity lines come in a new order: Monster Pierce, Minions of Destruction, your Attack/Cast Speed, Damage Reduction, Monster Cast/Attack Speed, Attack Rating and Pierce, Monster Defense, Chance to Block, and Physical Damage.
    • Filter Editor: in Alerts, How loud each kind of drop is counts the new Medium value Grail look and its sound with "Set items you haven't discovered".
    • IAS Calculator: skill cooldowns count. Held, Shock Web, and Plague Javelin wait a second between throws, and Dragon Flight and Joust for a time that shortens with their level (Alma Negra, Leoric's Mithril Bane, and Zerae's Resolve shorten Joust's). More IAS still helps: it brings the throw or strike sooner. The Damage Calculator counts them too.
    • A new layout for the IAS Calculator: on a wide screen, three columns: your setup, the answer with the breakpoints, and the attack preview, which stays in view while you scroll. On a narrower one the preview comes right under the answer. The answer's numbers sit side by side in one box, with the next breakpoints under it as rows (Next, Then, To spare). The breakpoints show your row with the two slower and five faster frame counts around it (ten in three columns); a button opens the rest.
    • Also on the IAS Calculator: the preview draws your buffs on the character as the game does: Fanaticism's flames under the feet, Frenzy's swirl, Burst of Speed (an Assassin's own or Hustle's), and the Whirlwind and Blade Dance spin, which now keeps turning from one hit to the next. The target is drawn in front of your weapon where a swing passes behind it, as in game. An attack that shoots or throws stands its target about 5 yards off, and its arrows, bolts, and javelins fly at the game's speed (they flew a third too fast); Double Throw's second throw flies too. Fire Blast's and Shock Web's bombs fly in a high arc, and they and exploding arrows are drawn where they land. Fire Blast's land as close to the target as in game: their spread goes by the points you put into it, not +skills. A skill that only an item gives is listed for the classes that can use that item, and picking it puts the item in your hand (Blade Dance: Chaos in a War Fist).
    • Common builds: the builds the IAS and Damage Calculators can start from are now the most played Season 14 builds, such as Zeal with Death Cleaver, Frenzy with Doombringer and The Grandfather, Fury werewolves with Tomb Reaver, and Strafe with Demon Machine, with the IAS, skill levels, and auras their players commonly have.
    • Run Speed Visualizer: each setup has a copy button, so you can copy a setup, change the copy, and compare the two. Clear setups starts you from one blank setup (with Undo), and the race is 30 yards by default (it was 50).
    • Monster Damage Calculator: T4 dungeons are rare only and roll only each affix's highest tier (Formidable 20-25%, Obliterating 35-45%), and a map takes at most 3 prefixes and 3 suffixes when rare, one of each when magic: past that the other affixes are grayed out.
    • The calculators share one look: the same button, list, and text sizes on all five. The Damage Calculator writes minus signs and thousands separators (+1,738%), Infinity Base Comparison's chart axes keep their decimals (27.5, not 28), and Ohm vs Jewel Comparison's table and answer line up.

    November 19th¶

    Season 14 - November 19th - v1. Ethereal bases to brick into rares, warnings on T4 dungeons and Treacherous maps, and a full review of the IAS Calculator.

    • Bases to brick: ethereal non-magical weapons with no sockets that your class would corrupt into a rare show with a red star after the name and the note "Corrupt this for a 50% Chance to Brick into a Rare". A Barbarian sees Mauls, Pikes, Glaives, and Short Spears, a Paladin War Hammers and Flails, an Amazon her javelins, an Assassin Hatchet Hands, and a Druid Great Axes, each with its exceptional and elite versions. They show on every filter level but 10 and 12, once the item level is high enough for a rare with six affixes that can roll 301-400% enhanced damage (85 to 87, depending on the base).
    • Ogre Mauls and Thunder Mauls, ethereal with no sockets, get a minimap icon and chat notification on levels 0 to 2 below character level 80.
    • T4 dungeons show the dangerous map warnings: Deadly Strike, Crushing Blow, Damage Reduction, Chance to Block, Physical Damage, Monster Cast/Attack Speed, Attack/Cast Speed, Hit Recovery, Drain Life, Max Resistances, All Resistances, Player Defense, and Dual, Triple, or Quad Extra Damage. They get no density or rarity lines, and no Attack Rating line: every dungeon has a preset +80% to +100%.
    • Treacherous maps show only the Deadly Strike, Crushing Blow, Damage Reduction, Chance to Block, Physical Damage, Monster Cast/Attack Speed, and Quad Extra Damage warnings, now at every strength, as on other maps. Their density, rarity, bonus, and "Map contains" lines are as before.
    • Map icons: a map shows a minimap icon and chat notification only where it can drop: non-magical T1-T3 maps and unique maps, outside town. Magic and rare maps and T4 dungeons, which you make yourself, no longer get one, and neither does any map in town.
    • Monster Cast/Attack Speed is one red line at every roll: purple from +70% is gone.
    • Melee Splash shows for both kinds of the Splashing affix: the stronger one, which rolls only on magic maps and is six times as common there, had no line.
    • Revealed versions: the slash between two names (Horizon's/Stormlash) is light gray instead of white.
    • Corrlorized versions: the Monster Rarity lines are back on, their number in sage.
    • Filter Editor: Features has a switch for the bases to brick, and under it you can pick your own: a tab per class, every weapon family it can use, on the filter levels you choose. Item changes can be limited to your class. The preview has a character of your own for the map lines that check yours (life, defense, block, hit recovery, resistances), set to endgame numbers you can change. In Maps, Warn and Mark write the line as the filter does (-120 Drain Life) next to the filter's own lines, density and rarity (already on every map's label) can be switched off, and a Melee Splash warning or hide works (it never matched). The preview's stat picker sorts the stats into sections, asks for a class, skill tab, or skill by name, and offers each element's damage as the game writes it.
    • IAS Calculator: under the attack preview, the animation's numbers end with how they make the frames: 18 × 256 ÷ 309 = 14.91, rounded up to 15, minus 1: 14 frames. The preview shows each frame as the game does and marks a hit on the first frame that shows it.
    • Lost hits: a held Strafe or Fend can lose hits. Slowed far enough, Strafe shoots only its first arrow and Fend hits once, and Fend with a javelin loses its third hit just short of its fastest speed. The breakpoints and the Damage Calculator count only the hits that land.
    • Also on the IAS Calculator: the most IAS a weapon can carry in werewolf or werebear form counts a unique's own sockets and cube upgrades (an Ettin Axe can carry 180: Rune Master). Blade Fury follows a 20-second count in game, a blade every 7 frames with no weapon and no IAS (it said 6). Hustle's Burst of Speed counts for mercenaries. Holy Freeze from Duriel, Uber Duriel, the Corrupted Paladin, and Shattered Soul slows by the right amount, here and on the Run Speed Visualizer. Fastest Frames lets you pick the attack.
    • The Grail Planner no longer says a Corrupted Ear can't go on a T4 dungeon.
    • The Monster Damage Calculator lets you corrupt a T4 dungeon too, with a dungeon's own rolls (deadly strike 15-20%).
    • The Non-magical Bases page draws the bases to brick with their star and note, and every note a base writes in its hover box under its label.

    November 17th¶

    Season 14 - November 17th - v1, v2. More map warnings check your character, and the experience tag helps you decide what to corrupt.

    • -X% Defense and +X% Attack Rating check the character hovering the map: red when at least 15% more of the monsters' hits land on you, purple from 50% more.
    • Attack rating and pierce come from one affix, so they get one line: "+X% Attack Rating" where your defense makes it a warning. On the 20 maps whose monsters fire missiles that can pierce, the line says "+X% Attack Rating & Pierce" there, and "+X% Monster Pierce" (red from +30%) where only the pierce matters.
    • Drain Life checks how long your life lasts against it: red under 150 seconds, purple under 90. With no Replenish Life, a character with 600 life sees red from −41 and purple from −69, one with 1,500 life from −103 and −171.
    • Dual Extra Damage is red on corrupted maps too when its two kinds are two of fire, cold, and lightning. +10% to +14% Physical Damage is red on uncorrupted maps (it was gray).
    • -X% Attack/Cast Speed goes by the map's number again (November 16th's v4 checked your character): red, purple at −20%.
    • Density: gold, red, and purple are the top half, the top fifth, and the top 5% of rolled rare maps, with corrupted maps' red and purple measured against corrupted maps. Low Monster Density marks the thinnest quarter of uncorrupted rare maps: density 55, 59, or 64 or less on T1, T2, or T3. Density, rarity, and the lines with both have new stars.
    • The experience tag (1.72x Experience) shows on uncorrupted maps only: it tells you whether a map's rolls are worth corrupting, and once the map is corrupted the "at Level" line tells you what it gives. Gold from 1.45x, 1.50x, or 1.55x on T1, T2, or T3, red from 1.65x, 1.70x, or 1.75x, purple from 1.80x, 1.85x, or 1.95x; every uncorrupted dungeon shows it, red from 2.65x and purple from 2.90x. Both numbers have two decimals.
    • Tiaras: unsocketed non-magical Tiaras of item level 85 and up show as non-magical Diadems do, since a rare corrupted from one rolls the same affixes. The note says a 50% chance to brick into a rare (it said 25%).
    • Filter Editor: the browser can't save into a game installed under Program Files, so the editor says so, downloads the filter instead, and remembers that.
    • Corrlorized versions: Corr's fifth round: more of his gold thresholds, his potion looks, his colors on more map lines.
    • v2: the map lines come in a new order, short ones at the top of the hover box, longer ones lower, and the star lines last, with shorter wordings: +X% Crafting (was Crafting Mats), X% Player Defense (was X% Defense), +X% Velocity (was Monster Velocity), Quad Extra Damage (was Quadruple), and +X% Attk.Rating/Pierce. Catalyzed is the last line, under the experience lines and next to the game's Corrupted, Treacherous, and Fortified lines.

    November 16th¶

    Season 14 - November 16th - v1 to v9. A map update: the highlights follow how maps really roll, and the warnings that matter most look at your own character.

    • Density and rarity have their own cuts on each tier, and corrupted maps are measured against corrupted maps. Gold, red, and purple each mean something on every kind of map: before, an uncorrupted map could hardly ever get past gold.
    • Low Monster Density marks about 1 in 8 uncorrupted rare maps, the thinnest ones (it marked about 2 in 5).
    • Chance to Block, Hit Recovery, and All Resistances check the character hovering the map: how many more hits land on you, how many hit recovery frames you lose, how much resistance you lose once what's over the cap has soaked the map's minus.
    • Most other map lines have new bands: small rolls no longer show, and red and purple are kept for the rolls that hurt. Unique maps count as corrupted, and get a tag line.
    • Removed: the players' +X% Attack/Cast Speed and +X% Jewelry/Charms bonuses. Ghosts, Fetishes, and Hell Bovines show on uncorrupted maps only.
    • Jealousy bases: ethereal Matriarchal Javelins with 0 or 3 sockets are never hidden.
    • Corrlorized versions: Corr's fourth round of changes.
    • v2, later the same day: Hit Recovery turns red from 2 frames lost and purple from 4, on every map.
    • v3: the experience tag colors only its number gold, red, or purple; the word Experience is gold, like the line under it.
    • v4: the -X% Attack/Cast Speed warning checks your character too: it shows when the map costs you a frame of cast speed (if you have at least as much FCR as IAS) or of attack speed (at least as much IAS as FCR), red for one frame and purple for two or more. Attack speed is worked out for a typical attack, as the filter can't see your weapon or skill.
    • v5: the "at Level" experience line colors its number too: red from 2.0x and purple from 2.5x. T4 dungeons, which are compared with the T3 maps, have their own: red from 10.0x and purple from 12.0x up to level 94, rising to 16.0x and 20.0x at level 98, so about 1 in 3 rare dungeons shows red or purple and 1 in 20 purple at any level.
    • v6: dungeons' "at Level" colors followed what a corrupted T3 map gets at your level: red from 9.0x and purple from 11.5x up to level 94, rising to 13.0x and 15.0x at level 98.
    • v7: dungeons can be corrupted too, so their colors now work like a T3 map's: an uncorrupted dungeon shows red or purple about as often as an uncorrupted T3 map, a corrupted one as often as a corrupted T3 map. The "at Level" line is red from 10.5x and purple from 13.0x up to level 94, rising to 14.5x and 18.0x at level 98. The Experience tag now shows on every dungeon: gold below 2.9x, red from 2.9x, and purple from 3.5x (it showed from 2.3x, red from 2.6x, and purple from 3.0x, which made most corrupted dungeons purple).
    • v8: the Monster Pierce warning shows at most +100%, since a higher chance does nothing more (some T3 maps roll up to +127%). The IAS Calculator's corrupted map tip gives the attack speed maps really keep: the game holds the stat at −20% at the lowest, so a T3 or T4 map's −25% to −35% corruption is always −20%. The Grail Planner adds T4 dungeons: what a whole dungeon and its bosses find, against maps in the same time.
    • v9: unidentified magic rings on level 2 show the ring's dots, with level 1's minimap icon and chat notification below character level 20 (level 2 showed only their name).
