    What's New¶

    What changed in each update of the filter, newest first. Your Horadric Cube's description shows the update you have, such as Season 14 - November 16th - v2, and the launcher brings each new one the next time you press Play. Since November 4th, a day with several updates numbers them: v1, v2 and so on.

    November 21st¶

    Nothing changes in the game: your Horadric Cube still shows Season 14 - November 20th - v2. The website gets a Feedback tab.

    • Feedback is a new tab: tell me when an item shows, hides, looks or sounds wrong, suggest a change to the filter or to a page of this site, or say what doesn't work on a page. A few short questions, sent as an anonymous Google Form submission, with an option to add your Discord name if you'd like a reply. Questions are still quickest on Discord: erazure.
    • A Feedback button on every page (the speech bubble at the top, and "Feedback on this page" at the bottom) opens it with what that page knows already filled in: the calculator setup you were looking at, the filter level you picked on the item pages, or the Filter Editor's item and your changes.
    • Feedback on these changes: "Give feedback on changes" at the top of this page lets you tick the changes you want to comment on, then send a comment on each.

    November 20th¶

    Season 14 - November 20th - v1 to v2. Medium value set items you haven't discovered show the Grail look, maps get a Monster Defense warning, the last map warnings come in a new order, and the IAS Calculator gets a new layout and counts skill cooldowns.

    • Medium value set items you haven't discovered get a Grail look of their own: the Grail look's four stars a side, the outermost orange, with the white dots by the name that mark Medium value, and the set Grail icon. Until now they showed their Medium value look whether you had found them or not, so a new Tancred's Hobnails looked like any other pair. Since v2 they also play the Grail drop sound instead of their own. The Minimal and Corrlorized versions draw the Grail stars with Medium value's white arrows. High value set items, and High and Medium value uniques, still look the same either way. The Unique & Set Tiers page and the Grail Planner say so.
    • +X% Monster Defense is a new map warning for characters who attack: it says how much of your chance to hit the map's monsters take away. Red when you lose 10 points or more (95% down to 85%, say), purple from 20. It goes by your level, dexterity, attack rating and +% attack rating on gear, with the attack skill bonus a typical build of your class has (the filter can't see your skill or weapon mastery), and by each map's own monsters: 14 maps' monsters are noticeably easier or harder to hit than most. Casters don't see it: a character with more faster cast rate than increased attack speed on gear (an Amazon, Assassin or Barbarian may have up to 30 more), or with a low attack rating, or with Ignore Target's Defense. T1-T3 and unique maps, corrupted too, not Treacherous maps. The suffixes that bring it (+50% to +200% on about 1 rare map in 7, up to +300% on magic ones) also bring experience and magic find.
    • Map warnings: the lines between All Resistances and the density and rarity lines come in a new order: Monster Pierce, Minions of Destruction, your Attack/Cast Speed, Damage Reduction, Monster Cast/Attack Speed, Attack Rating and Pierce, Monster Defense, Chance to Block and Physical Damage.
    • Filter Editor: in Alerts, How loud each kind of drop is counts the new Medium value Grail look and its sound with "Set items you haven't discovered".
    • IAS Calculator: skill cooldowns count. Held, Shock Web and Plague Javelin wait a second between throws, and Dragon Flight and Joust for a time that shortens with their level (Alma Negra, Leoric's Mithril Bane and Zerae's Resolve shorten Joust's). More IAS still helps: it brings the throw or strike sooner. The Damage Calculator counts them too.
    • A new layout for the IAS Calculator: on a wide screen, three columns: your setup, the answer with the breakpoints, and the attack preview, which stays in view while you scroll. On a narrower one the preview comes right under the answer. The answer's numbers sit side by side in one box, with the next breakpoints under it as rows (Next, Then, To spare). The breakpoints show your row with the two slower and five faster frame counts around it (ten in three columns); a button opens the rest.
    • Also on the IAS Calculator: the preview draws your buffs on the character as the game does: Fanaticism's flames under the feet, Frenzy's swirl, Burst of Speed (an Assassin's own or Hustle's) and the Whirlwind and Blade Dance spin, which now keeps turning from one hit to the next. The target is drawn in front of your weapon where a swing passes behind it, as in game. An attack that shoots or throws stands its target about 5 yards off, and its arrows, bolts and javelins fly at the game's speed (they flew a third too fast); Double Throw's second throw flies too. Fire Blast's and Shock Web's bombs fly in a high arc, and they and exploding arrows are drawn where they land. Fire Blast's land as close to the target as in game: their spread goes by the points you put into it, not +skills. A skill that only an item gives is listed for the classes that can use that item, and picking it puts the item in your hand (Blade Dance: Chaos in a War Fist).
    • Common builds: the builds the IAS and Damage Calculators can start from are now the most played Season 14 builds, such as Zeal with Death Cleaver, Frenzy with Doombringer and The Grandfather, Fury werewolves with Tomb Reaver and Strafe with Demon Machine, with the IAS, skill levels and auras their players commonly have.
    • Run Speed Visualizer: each setup has a copy button, so you can copy a setup, change the copy and compare the two. Clear setups starts you from one blank setup (with Undo), and the race is 30 yards by default (it was 50).
    • Monster Damage Calculator: T4 dungeons are rare only and roll only each affix's highest tier (Formidable 20-25%, Obliterating 35-45%), and a map takes at most 3 prefixes and 3 suffixes when rare, one of each when magic: past that the other affixes are greyed out.
    • The calculators share one look: the same button, list and text sizes on all five. The Damage Calculator writes minus signs and thousands separators (+1,738%), Infinity Base Comparison's chart axes keep their decimals (27.5, not 28), and Ohm vs Jewel Comparison's table and answer line up.

    November 19th¶

    Season 14 - November 19th - v1. Ethereal bases to brick into rares, warnings on T4 dungeons and Treacherous maps, and a full review of the IAS Calculator.

    • Bases to brick: ethereal non-magical weapons with no sockets that your class would corrupt into a rare show with a red star after the name and the note "Corrupt this for a 50% Chance to Brick into a Rare". A Barbarian sees Mauls, Pikes, Glaives and Short Spears, a Paladin War Hammers and Flails, an Amazon her javelins, an Assassin Hatchet Hands and a Druid Great Axes, each with its exceptional and elite versions. They show on every filter level but 10 and 12, once the item level is high enough for a rare with six affixes that can roll 301-400% enhanced damage (85 to 87, depending on the base).
    • Ogre Mauls and Thunder Mauls, ethereal with no sockets, get a minimap icon and chat notification on levels 0 to 2 below character level 80.
    • T4 dungeons show the dangerous map warnings: Deadly Strike, Crushing Blow, Damage Reduction, Chance to Block, Physical Damage, Monster Cast/Attack Speed, Attack/Cast Speed, Hit Recovery, Drain Life, Max Resistances, All Resistances, Player Defense and Dual, Triple or Quad Extra Damage. They get no density or rarity lines, and no Attack Rating line: the Dungeon Scarab gives every dungeon +80% to +100%.
    • Treacherous maps show only the Deadly Strike, Crushing Blow, Damage Reduction, Chance to Block, Physical Damage, Monster Cast/Attack Speed and Quad Extra Damage warnings, now at every strength, as on other maps. Their density, rarity, bonus and "Map contains" lines are as before.
    • Map icons: a map shows a minimap icon and chat notification only where it can drop: non-magical T1-T3 maps and unique maps, outside town. Magic and rare maps and T4 dungeons, which you make yourself, no longer get one, and neither does any map in town.
    • Monster Cast/Attack Speed is one red line at every roll: purple from +70% is gone.
    • Melee Splash shows for both kinds of the Splashing affix: the stronger one, which rolls only on magic maps and is six times as common there, had no line.
    • Revealed versions: the slash between two names (Horizon's/Stormlash) is light gray instead of white.
    • Corrlorized versions: the Monster Rarity lines are back on, their number in sage.
    • Filter Editor: Features has a switch for the bases to brick, and under it you can pick your own: a tab per class, every weapon family it can use, on the filter levels you choose. Item changes can be limited to your class. The preview has a character of your own for the map lines that check yours (life, defense, block, hit recovery, resistances), set to endgame numbers you can change. In Maps, Warn and Mark write the line as the filter does (-120 Drain Life) next to the filter's own lines, density and rarity (already on every map's label) can be switched off, and a Melee Splash warning or hide works (it never matched). The preview's stat picker sorts the stats into sections, asks for a class, skill tab or skill by name, and offers each element's damage as the game writes it.
    • IAS Calculator: under the attack preview, the animation's numbers end with how they make the frames: 18 × 256 ÷ 309 = 14.91, rounded up to 15, minus 1: 14 frames. The preview shows each frame as the game does and marks a hit on the first frame that shows it.
    • Lost hits: a held Strafe or Fend can lose hits. Slowed far enough, Strafe shoots only its first arrow and Fend hits once, and Fend with a javelin loses its third hit just short of its fastest speed. The breakpoints and the Damage Calculator count only the hits that land.
    • Also on the IAS Calculator: the most IAS a weapon can carry in werewolf or werebear form counts a unique's own sockets and cube upgrades (an Ettin Axe can carry 180: Rune Master). Blade Fury follows a 20-second count in game, a blade every 7 frames with no weapon and no IAS (it said 6). Hustle's Burst of Speed counts for mercenaries. Holy Freeze from Duriel, Uber Duriel, the Corrupted Paladin and Shattered Soul slows by the right amount, here and on the Run Speed Visualizer. Fastest Frames lets you pick the attack.
    • The Grail Planner no longer says a Corrupted Ear can't go on a T4 dungeon.
    • The Monster Damage Calculator lets you corrupt a T4 dungeon too, with a dungeon's own rolls (deadly strike 15-20%).
    • The Non-magical Bases page draws the bases to brick with their star and note, and every note a base writes in its hover box under its label.

    November 17th¶

    Season 14 - November 17th - v1, v2. More map warnings check your character, and the experience tag helps you decide what to corrupt.

    • -X% Defense and +X% Attack Rating check the character hovering the map: red when at least 15% more of the monsters' hits land on you, purple from 50% more.
    • Attack rating and pierce come from one affix, so they get one line: "+X% Attack Rating" where your defense makes it a warning. On the 20 maps whose monsters fire missiles that can pierce, the line says "+X% Attack Rating & Pierce" there, and "+X% Monster Pierce" (red from +30%) where only the pierce matters.
    • Drain Life checks how long your life lasts against it: red under 150 seconds, purple under 90. With no Replenish Life, a character with 600 life sees red from -41 and purple from -69, one with 1,500 life from -103 and -171.
    • Dual Extra Damage is red on corrupted maps too when its two kinds are two of fire, cold and lightning. +10% to +14% Physical Damage is red on uncorrupted maps (it was gray).
    • -X% Attack/Cast Speed goes by the map's number again (November 16th's v4 checked your character): red, purple at -20%.
    • Density: gold, red and purple are the top half, the top fifth and the top 5% of rolled rare maps, with corrupted maps' red and purple measured against corrupted maps. Low Monster Density marks the thinnest quarter of uncorrupted rare maps: density 55, 59 or 64 or less on T1, T2 or T3. Density, rarity and the lines with both have new stars.
    • The experience tag (1.72x Experience) shows on uncorrupted maps only: it tells you whether a map's rolls are worth corrupting, and once the map is corrupted the "at Level" line tells you what it gives. Gold from 1.45x, 1.50x or 1.55x on T1, T2 or T3, red from 1.65x, 1.70x or 1.75x, purple from 1.80x, 1.85x or 1.95x; every uncorrupted dungeon shows it, red from 2.65x and purple from 2.90x. Both numbers have two decimals.
    • Tiaras: unsocketed non-magical Tiaras of item level 85 and up show as non-magical Diadems do, since a rare corrupted from one rolls the same affixes. The note says a 50% chance to brick into a rare (it said 25%).
    • Filter Editor: the browser can't save into a game installed under Program Files, so the editor says so, downloads the filter instead and remembers that.
    • Corrlorized versions: Corr's fifth round: more of his gold thresholds, his potion looks, his colors on more map lines.
    • v2: the map lines come in a new order, short ones at the top of the hover box, longer ones lower and the star lines last, with shorter wordings: +X% Crafting (was Crafting Mats), X% Player Defense (was X% Defense), +X% Velocity (was Monster Velocity), Quad Extra Damage (was Quadruple) and +X% Attk.Rating/Pierce. Catalyzed is the last line, under the experience lines and next to the game's Corrupted, Treacherous and Fortified lines.

    November 16th¶

    Season 14 - November 16th - v1 to v9. A map update: the highlights follow how maps really roll, and the warnings that matter most look at your own character.

    • Density and rarity have their own cuts on each tier, and corrupted maps are measured against corrupted maps. Gold, red and purple each mean something on every kind of map: before, an uncorrupted map could hardly ever get past gold.
    • Low Monster Density marks about 1 in 8 uncorrupted rare maps, the thinnest ones (it marked about 2 in 5).
    • Chance to Block, Hit Recovery and All Resistances check the character hovering the map: how many more hits land on you, how many hit recovery frames you lose, how much resistance you lose once what's over the cap has soaked the map's minus.
    • Most other map lines have new bands: small rolls no longer show, and red and purple are kept for the rolls that hurt. Unique maps count as corrupted, and get a tag line.
    • Removed: the players' +X% Attack/Cast Speed and +X% Jewelry/Charms bonuses. Ghosts, Fetishes and Hell Bovines show on uncorrupted maps only.
    • Jealousy bases: ethereal Matriarchal Javelins with 0 or 3 sockets are never hidden.
    • Corrlorized versions: Corr's fourth round of changes.
    • v2, later the same day: Hit Recovery turns red from 2 frames lost and purple from 4, on every map.
    • v3: the experience tag colors only its number gold, red or purple; the word Experience is gold, like the line under it.
    • v4: the -X% Attack/Cast Speed warning checks your character too: it shows when the map costs you a frame of cast speed (if you have at least as much FCR as IAS) or of attack speed (at least as much IAS as FCR), red for one frame and purple for two or more. Attack speed is worked out for a typical attack, as the filter can't see your weapon or skill.
    • v5: the "at Level" experience line colors its number too: red from 2.0x and purple from 2.5x. T4 dungeons, which are compared with the T3 maps, have their own: red from 10.0x and purple from 12.0x up to level 94, rising to 16.0x and 20.0x at level 98, so about 1 in 3 rare dungeons shows red or purple and 1 in 20 purple at any level.
    • v6: dungeons' "at Level" colors followed what a corrupted T3 map gets at your level: red from 9.0x and purple from 11.5x up to level 94, rising to 13.0x and 15.0x at level 98.
    • v7: dungeons can be corrupted too, so their colors now work like a T3 map's: an uncorrupted dungeon shows red or purple about as often as an uncorrupted T3 map, a corrupted one as often as a corrupted T3 map. The "at Level" line is red from 10.5x and purple from 13.0x up to level 94, rising to 14.5x and 18.0x at level 98. The Experience tag now shows on every dungeon: gold below 2.9x, red from 2.9x and purple from 3.5x (it showed from 2.3x, red from 2.6x and purple from 3.0x, which made most corrupted dungeons purple).
    • v8: the Monster Pierce warning shows at most +100%, since a higher chance does nothing more (some T3 maps roll up to +127%). The IAS Calculator's corrupted map tip gives the attack speed maps really keep: the game holds the stat at -20% at the lowest, so a T3 or T4 map's -25% to -35% corruption is always -20%. The Grail Planner adds T4 dungeons: what a whole dungeon and its bosses find, against maps in the same time.
    • v9: unidentified magic rings on level 2 show the ring's dots, with level 1's minimap icon and chat notification below character level 20 (level 2 showed only their name).

    November 13th¶

    Season 14 - November 13th - v1. Nothing changes in the game; the website grows.

    • The new IAS Calculator: attack speed breakpoints for every class, form, weapon and mercenary, with the attack drawn as the game draws it.
    • A race on the Run Speed Visualizer, clearer layouts for every calculator, the site grouped into four tabs, and a preview card when you post a page's link.

    November 12th¶

    Season 14 - November 12th - v1 to v3.

    • The Filter Editor is open: make your own changes to Erazure (looks, sounds, items, levels, rules of your own), see them as the game draws them, and save them straight into your game.
    • New calculators: the Damage Calculator, the Ohm vs Jewel Comparison and the Run Speed Visualizer.
    • Unique and set items whose sockets roll in a range show the most they can roll after their socket count: Hellrack 4/5.
    • Level 0 shows the healing and mana potions' labels, and Super Healing and Super Mana Potions show theirs in town too.
    • The 700% and up Enhanced Damage highlight has its own stars: green, teal, blue and purple.

    November 10th¶

    Season 14 - November 10th - v1.

    • Small non-magical items that sell well (daggers, wands, most orbs) show their sell tag, ($), on levels 1 to 4 where they used to be hidden.
    • Enhanced Damage highlights come in tiers, with a new tier from 700%; rare and crafted daggers get the 250% highlight.
    • Ethereal +3 Matriarchal Javelins with 0 or 3 sockets show an orange Jealousy tag.

    November 9th¶

    Season 14 - November 9th - v1. Druid pelts highlight +5 to +7 Shock Wave, Fire Claws, Maul and Fury, and clubs +5 to +7 Shock Wave.

    November 8th¶

    Season 14 - November 8th - v1 to v4.

    • The second set item review: 65 set item bases look different on some filter levels.
    • Naj's Puzzler is Medium value on levels 0 to 7, the set look with a small icon on 8 to 11, its plain name on 12.
    • The Amplify Damage and Lower Resist highlights sit under the Enhanced Damage highlight.
    • Magic class items of your own class show the ring's dots around their name.
    • Maces and scepters show their damage to undead with the game's own +50% added, as their hover box does.

    November 7th¶

    Season 14 - November 7th - v1, v2.

    • Staves tag a chance to cast Charged Bolt, Lightning, Nova, Chain Lightning, Fire Ball, Frost Nova or Ice Barrage when casting, with the skill's level: 24fbl.
    • Rare and crafted staves, wands and orbs no longer tag life, single resistances or magic damage reduction from affix level 60; clubs, orbs, wands and staves tag single resistances and energy below affix level 60 only.

    • Larzuk's Puzzlebox and Puzzlepiece list their sockets in gold.

    Season 14 - November 6th - v1. Ethereal elite spears with 3 sockets show on levels 3 and 4 at every character level, as elite polearms do.

    November 5th¶

    Season 14 - November 5th - v1 to v5.

    • Every unique and set item you haven't discovered yet plays a drop sound.
    • Medium value quivers have drop sounds of their own, and Snakecord joins the Minimal value uniques.
    • Map experience: the "at Level" line walks each map from where you arrive and rounds to the nearest tenth, and T3 maps' experience tags are a tenth lower.

    Season 14 - November 4th, then v1, v2.

    • Level 12 hides more gold: from character level 80 under 10,000, from 85 under 15,000, from 90 under 20,000 and from 95 under 25,000.
    • Levels 10 and 12 hide every unidentified magic Ward, Aegis and Monarch.

    Season 14 - November 3rd. Ethereal magic Boneweave leaves the Jeweler's chest look, and Gloom's Trap leaves the Minimal value tier.

    November 2nd¶

    Season 14 - November 2nd. What the filter levels show, levels 3 to 12 most of all.

    • Unidentified rare gloves look the same for every class. Rare gloves and Paladin shields get a lower look on levels 8 to 11: tan dots, a small minimap icon, no chat notification.
    • Unidentified normal-tier rare swords, axes, spears, polearms, javelins and throwing weapons show on levels 3 to 7.
    • Unidentified rare arrows and bolts get gray dots around their name and the small minimap icon.
    • Non-magical bases: levels 5 to 7 hide those under 10% enhanced damage (8 to 12 keep 15%). Elite one-handed swords (but Phase Blades) and axes hide on levels 8 to 12, and so do normal and exceptional chests with 19 to 20% enhanced damage (but Mage Plate).
    • Undiscovered set items get the Grail look on level 12; Sage's Defiance is Medium value on levels 0 to 7.
    • Magic Jared's Stones, Swirling Crystals and Dimensional Shards show for Sorceresses on levels 5 to 7.
    • Affix tags come in the order the game lists the stats, and the Rathma, D.Clone and Lucion tags on rare helms and belts were tuned.
    • New on the website: the Resources tab, with Map Experience and the Infinity Base Comparison.

    • A yellow star joins the unique and set Grail looks, and undiscovered set rings and amulets get the set Grail look.
    • Season 14's 18 alternate-skin uniques get the orange "5th Anniversary Edition" line.
    • Rare arrows and bolts notify.

    • Set amulets keep the set look with a small icon on levels 5 to 7 at character level 90 and up.

    October 31st¶

    Season 14 - October 31st.

    • The first set item review: 52 set item bases look different on some filter levels.
    • Max resistance tags lose the percent sign: 5max.
    • Ethereal magic Ancient Armor, Full Plate Mail and Gothic Plate, and their exceptional and elite versions, show as crafting bases with a minimap icon.
    • Small and Grand Charms below item level 90 keep the regular charm look on levels 5 to 7.
    • Non-magical staves, wands, claws and class items show their skill tags while you wear them.
    • Corrupted weapons show Amplify Damage and Lower Resist without stars, rare helms with at most 2 sockets show on levels 5 to 7, and Milabrega's Orb shows on levels 3 to 7.
    • New on the website: the Grail Planner.

    Every set item that changed, before and after

    October 30th¶

    Season 14 - October 30th.

    • Quivers: below affix level 60, magic, rare and crafted quivers tag every roll of the stats that matter, and identified quivers with too few useful stats hide from character level 30.
    • Corrupted items: +N to one skill highlights drop their stars, rare and crafted weapons drop their minimum and maximum damage tags, and weapons drop their -% enemy resistance tags (socketed ones too), so the hover box keeps its room.
    • Non-magical Shadow Bows with 0 or 6 sockets notify.
    • Innocence shows its Blade Shield level and enhanced defense.
    • Level 1 hides normal gems and skulls from character level 60 (was 80).
    • In the launcher, Erazure - BIG GG and Minimal - BIG GG are now called BIG GG - Mystery.

    October 29th¶

    Season 14 - October 29th.

    • The unique and set tier review, after trade prices: 26 tier changes.
    • Level 1 shows every non-magical chest, helm, circlet, shield, weapon and quiver with 2 or more sockets, at every character level.
    • The lines under an item's name come in one order: boss tags, skill and shop highlights, resistance tags, Enhanced Damage, Amplify Damage and Lower Resist, Damage Reduction, Max Resistances, Cannot Be Frozen.
    • Undiscovered unique and set items get minimap icons of their own.
    • Corrupted rare and crafted gloves and boots drop a few minor tags, so their labels fit.
    • Weapons tag attack rating, leech and open wounds damage only below affix level 60, and damage to demons and undead from 60 only on weapons with 100% enhanced damage or more.

    Every unique and set item that changed, before and after

    October 28th¶

    Season 14 - October 28th.

    • Low-level rare tags: below affix level 60, rare and crafted items tag every roll of the stats that matter for their item type.
    • Rare and crafted weapons tag minimum and maximum damage as one tag, 1-3dmg, and more stats while nothing is socketed and they aren't corrupted.
    • The sell tag, ($), skips rares that already show 6 or more tags.
    • The Tri and Dual Resist lines are back on levels 5 to 12 for boots, belts and gloves.
    • Map experience: the "at Level" line counts a full clear per tile walked, and Horazon's Memory leaves out its lightning spires.
    • Rathma's Voidstones show All Resistances overcap 34 / 38 / 42.

    October 27th¶

    Season 14 - October 27th.

    • Two new versions in the launcher: Corrlorized - Revealed - PoE and Corrlorized - Revealed - BIG GG - PoE.
    • The +2 class skills highlight on body armor and shields needs two of cast rate, resistances and damage reduction.

    October 26th¶

    Season 14 - October 26th.

    • Level 9 shows Flawless Gems; levels 8 and 10 to 12 hide them.
