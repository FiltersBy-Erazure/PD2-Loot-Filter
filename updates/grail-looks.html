Grail Looks and Sounds

Season 14 - November 1st to 5th. A unique or set item you haven't discovered yet (the stash's Compendium counts it as undiscovered) shows a look of its own on the ground, the Grail look, so a new one stands out from one you already have. It is never hidden. These updates changed the Grail looks and gave every undiscovered item a drop sound:

November 1st
A yellow star joins both Grail looks, and the unique one's third star turns tan beside the gold name. Set rings and amulets get the set Grail look on every filter level; until then they had their set look with the Grail minimap icon (from character level 80, rings on level 12 had only the icon and chat notification, and so did amulets on levels 8 to 12 from character level 90).
November 2nd
Filter level 12: the set items of High or Medium value that show only their name there (18 of them) get the Grail look; until then their name, with the Grail minimap icon and chat notification.
November 4th
Minimal value uniques get a Grail look of their own: the Minimal value look in the Grail look's colors, with a star beside the name, and the Grail minimap icon. Until then they looked like the ones you have.
November 5th
Every undiscovered unique or set item plays a drop sound: an item with a sound of its own (High and Medium value) keeps it, every other one plays the Grail sound. Corrlorized's versions play no Grail sound.

Before and after

An undiscovered item of each kind, unidentified on the ground outside town, in Erazure - Main on filter level 1 (level 12 where it says): its look, minimap icon, chat notification and drop sound (▶ plays it). The Minimal versions draw the Grail looks with their own symbols (see the Minimal Version page), and the Grail Planner's Filter Display tab shows today's looks.
