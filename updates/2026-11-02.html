November 2nd Update

Filter level

Season 14 - November 2nd. This update changed what every filter level shows, levels 3 to 12 most of all. Pick a level in the bar: every unidentified item whose display changed on it is listed, before (November 1st) and after (November 2nd).

  • Unidentified rare gloves look the same for every class. Rare gloves and Paladin shields get a lower look on levels 8 to 11: tan dots, a small minimap icon, no chat notification.
  • Unidentified normal-tier rare swords, axes, spears, polearms, javelins, and throwing weapons show on levels 3 to 7.
  • Unidentified rare arrows and bolts get gray dots around their name and the small minimap icon.
  • Non-magical bases: levels 5 to 7 hide those under 10% enhanced damage (8 to 12 keep 15%). Elite one-handed swords (but Phase Blades) and axes hide on levels 8 to 12, and so do normal and exceptional chests with 19 to 20% enhanced damage (but Mage Plate).
  • Undiscovered set items get the Grail look on level 12 (the Grail looks page shows it); Sage's Defiance is Medium value on levels 0 to 7.
  • Magic Jared's Stones, Swirling Crystals, and Dimensional Shards show for Sorceresses on levels 5 to 7.

Each row is an unidentified drop on the ground outside town, in Erazure - Main, as the Rare Items, Magic Items, Non-magical Bases, and Unique & Set Tiers pages read the filter's rules: when it hides, shows, or notifies, with its look, minimap icon, chat notification, and drop sound (▶ plays it). A case no row covers shows the item's plain name, without a minimap icon. Items that changed alike on a level share a row.
