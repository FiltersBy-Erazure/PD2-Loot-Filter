    Season 14 Launch¶

    Everything that changed from the Season 13 filter to the one Season 14 started with, Season 14 - October 19th. The updates since then, from the ladder start on October 23rd, are on What's New, and some of them change what this page describes (the map lines, for one).

    Highlights¶

    1. Roll tags on uniques and sets. Identified unique and set items show their important variable rolls as short tags under the name, in the order the game lists them, as magic and rare items already did. I picked the rolls for all 505 unique and set items. A corrupted item shows each stat once. Where a label would run past the game's 56 characters, that item drops a roll, shortens its name, or joins two skill tags.
    2. Roll tags on runewords. 94 runewords show their variable rolls on a line under the name and base, with the runes' bonus included. Enigma shows its defense and its Strength and Magic Find per level; Call to Arms its three skill levels.
    3. Map experience lines. Identified maps show two lines in their description. The first says how much the map's rolls raise a full clear's experience (2.0x Experience), in gold, red, or purple by how good that is for its tier. The second compares the map with an average non-magical map at your character level (2.6x at Level 98, for characters of level 80 to 98). Map Experience explains both.
    4. Boss gear tags. Identified rare and crafted gear, and magic amulets, name every boss they suit: Rathma / D.Clone / Lucion. Season 13 tagged Rathma gear only (Rathma Amulet).
    5. New versions. Minimal, Minimal - Revealed, Minimal - BIG GG, and Minimal - PoE: Erazure with fewer, simpler symbols around item names, and the same filter levels, hiding, notifications, and sounds (see the labels). Corrlorized - Main - PoE and Corrlorized - BIG GG - PoE: Corr's restyle, with his own colors, symbols, and Path of Exile sounds. Versions has every version.
    6. Discovery Progress. The filter never hides a unique or set item you haven't discovered yet, and one in no value tier shows the Grail look until you have (orange stars). Once you have discovered every item it could be, it hides as before.
    7. New filter levels. Advanced Filtering (levels 5 to 7) sits between Stricter and Endgame. The levels that hid H5 and M5 are gone: the game's own Hide Healing Potions and Hide Mana Potions options do that now, on every level.
    8. The website. Six pages, read from the filter's own rules, show what every filter level does and how every unique, set, rare, magic, and non-magical item looks.

    Filter levels¶

    Every Season 13 level has a Season 14 match. Levels 5 to 7 are new, and the potion-only levels are gone: the game's own potion options do that job now.

    Season 14 level Name in the game Use it if you played on (Season 13)
    1 Lowest Strictness 1
    2 Relaxed Filtering 2
    3 Stricter Filtering 3, or 4 to 6 (Hides H5 / M5) with the game's potion options on
    4 Stricter Filtering - Hides 35% 7 (Hides H5, M5, 35%) with the potion options on
    5 Advanced Filtering New
    6 Advanced Filtering - Hides 35% New
    7 Advanced Filtering - Hides 35%, Amulets, Large Charms New
    8 Endgame Filtering - Hides 35% 8 or 9
    9 Endgame Filtering - Hides 35%, Large Charms New: level 8 that also hides Large Charms
    10 Endgame Filtering - Hides 35%, Crafting Bases 10
    11 Endgame Filtering - Hides 35%, Flawless Gems, Large Charms 11
    12 Extreme Filtering! - Hides 35%, Crafting Bases, Flawless Gems, Large Charms 12

    Each pair came out closest when every Season 13 level was compared with every Season 14 level over 3,303 kinds of drops, at character levels 85 and 95.

    • Advanced Filtering (5 to 7) is like Endgame but shows more rares. Levels 5 and 6 also show magic amulets and Large Charms; level 7 hides unidentified magic amulets and every unidentified Large Charm.
    • Level 9 is level 8 that also hides every unidentified Large Charm. Levels 8 and 10 still show Large Charms of item level 90 and up.
    • Potions: turn on Hide Healing Potions or Hide Mana Potions in the game's Loot Filter options to hide those potions on every level. The filter itself shows Super Healing and Super Mana Potions up to level 7 and hides them from level 8 (character level 50 and up).
    • 35% Rejuvenation Potions are hidden on levels 4 and 6 to 12, the levels whose names say so. There, Full Rejuvenation Potions get a bigger look.
    • The Horadric Cube: on level 0 its description sums up each group of levels in one line; on the other levels it shows the level's number and name.

    The ladder start's updates changed levels 8 and 9 (October 25th: level 8 shows Select Weapons) and Flawless Gems (October 26th): see What's New.

    Season 14 content¶

    The filter knows everything Season 14 adds to the game.

    • New maps: Poisoned Well (Tier 1), Dark Temple and Descent from Mount Arreat (Tier 2) have their names, tiers, resistances, and map highlights. Dark Temple shows its Cursing Knights; Descent its Succubus Witches.
    • New runewords: Finesse (3-socket body armor), Deception (3-socket helms), Madness (4-socket weapons), and Jealousy (3-socket Matriarchal Javelins) appear in the runeword lists of their runes and bases, and show their roll tags. Ethereal Madness and Jealousy bases show on levels 0 to 4, and with 15% or more enhanced damage on levels 5 to 12; ethereal +3 Matriarchal Javelins with 0 or 3 sockets notify.
    • Runeword lists on runes: Rapture, Kingslayer, Innocence, Flickering Flame, Wisdom, Asylum, Destruction, and Zenith are in, and the lists are re-ordered to fit the tooltip.
    • New uniques: Spectral Plate (High value on levels 0 to 7, Medium on 8 to 12) and Warriv's Warder (Minimal value).
    • 3 sockets: Splint Mail, Plate Mail, Field Plate, Ghost Armor, Serpentskin Armor, Demonhide Armor, Trellised Armor, Armet, Giant Conch, and Matriarchal Javelin now hold 3 sockets. Their non-magical bases are tagged like the other 3-socket bases, and the corruption notes say C:3.
    • Stacks: runes, gems, and skulls drop and stack as one item, and every gem stack shows its count (x 3). Stacks have plural names: Relics of the Ancients, Visions of Terror, Voidstones, Shadows of Hatred, Vials of Lightsong, Lilith's Mirrors, Tokens of Absolution, and Pandemonium Talismans.
    • Cube recipes: the rune, gem, and Jewel Fragment notes no longer ask for a key (Cube El Rune x 3 = Eld Rune).
    • Corruptions: the old Indestructible corruption now repairs durability: a gray Rep tag beside its enhanced damage. Half freeze duration (boots, gloves, belts) and curse duration on chests are tagged.
    • Jewel prefixes: the new +% elemental and magic skill damage prefixes are tagged on jewels in the Large Charm style (5%dmg in the element's color).
    • Affix levels: the best rare tier on chests, shields, and Paladin shields is affix level 75 now (Guardianship, Rampaging, Immortal's, the +2 class prefixes), so the filter's 85+ rules are 75+: a rare Sacred Armor shows from item level 87.
    • Curse procs: Amplify Damage and Lower Resist proc tags show the Season 14 skill levels.
    • Guild Halls count as town.
    • Holy Water Sprinkler shows as Holy Sprinkler, so its labels fit.
    • Rejuvenation Potions stack in the stash, where their names now show on every level.

    Rare, magic, and non-magical items¶

    Season 14's new rare affixes made some bases worth more, so rare helms and shields are decided per base. Everything here is for unidentified drops outside town; Magic Items, Rare Items, and Non-magical Bases list every base.

    Rare items

    • 2-socket helms (Cap, Skull Cap, Helm, Full Helm, Bone Helm, and their upgrades) show on levels 3 and 4 and hide from level 5. Season 13 hid them from level 3.
    • 3-socket helms (Mask, Great Helm, Crown, Bone Visage families, and now Armet and Giant Conch) hide only on levels 10 and 12. Season 13 hid them from level 8.
    • 3- and 4-socket shields (Large, Kite, Tower, Gothic, Spiked, and Bone Shield families) show on levels 10 and 12 as their plain name with a small minimap dot. Season 13 hid them there.
    • Rare class items you can use stay shown on level 10: your own class's, a Sorceress's staves and orbs, and for every class ethereal Amazon javelins and spears and the Reflex, Ceremonial, and Grand Matron Bows. From level 8, Barbarian helms hide for other classes, and so do Stag, Ashwood, and Matriarchal Bows and non-ethereal Amazon javelins.
    • Level 0 notifies rare class items for your own class below character level 50, as level 1 does.

    Magic items

    • Chests of any tier that can be crafted to the best affix level get the craft look on levels 0 to 4 (Season 13: elite chests only).
    • Shields that can hold 3 sockets, and Bone Shields, get that look too, and on levels 1 to 4 they stay shown at item level 94 and up (ethereal ones stay hidden on 2 to 4).
    • Amulets hide from level 7; Large Charms hide on levels 7, 9, 11, and 12, and below item level 90 on 8 and 10.

    Non-magical items

    • New 3-socket bases: 3-socket Ghost, Serpentskin, Demonhide, and Trellised Armor are tagged like Cuirass and Mesh Armor; 3-socket Armet and Giant Conch (Deception bases) like Spired Helm and Corona.
    • Enhanced damage, enhanced defense, and resistance tags sit on a line of their own under the name (they used to sit in front of it), in the game's order, as on magic items.
    • A runeword's base line drops its Sup, Inf, and enhanced damage tags, so the name and roll tags fit.
    • Non-magical weapons with high enhanced damage that no socket or level rule covered get their minimap icon.
    • Plague orbs show with their tag on every level, throwing knives follow the throwing weapon rules, Leaf staves stop being tagged where staves are hidden, Neophyte claw bases are recognized again, and Phase Blades with 3 or 5 sockets show on levels 0 to 2.
    • Gheed's gamble lines list the new uniques on their bases (Spectral Plate on Breast Plate, Warriv's Warder on Buckler).

    Maps¶

    Identified maps tell you what they are worth: a tag line under the name and two experience lines in the description. The November 16th update reworked the map lines since.

    • Tag line: a Tier 1 to 3 map's roll tags moved from the front of its name to a line of their own under it, 25exp 40rar 85den 120mf (experience, rarity, monster density, magic find), each colored by how good it is for the map's tier.
    • 2.0x Experience: how much the map's rolls raise a full clear's experience (density, rarity, +experience, +area level, Fortification, a guest monster), in gold, red, or purple by how it ranks among rolled rare maps of its tier; no line for the weakest 10%.
    • 2.6x at Level 98: what a full clear of this map gives your character, against an average non-magical Tier 1 to 3 map at your level (Tier 4 dungeons: against Tier 3), for characters of level 80 to 98.
    • Treacherous maps (cubed with a Corrupted Ear) skip the lines that don't matter there, and their density and rarity lines show without the stars.
    • Corrupted maps no longer show the extra boss, the +% armor and weapon drop, the elemental and physical damage, Hit Recovery, and Monster Pierce lines.
    • Dark Temple shows the lightning damage line (its boss casts a lightning nova), Poisoned Well the poison one.
    • Resistances follow Season 14: Throne of Insanity's magic resistance is 50, Kyovashad has new immunities (magic 105, lightning 120, fire 120), and guest monsters add theirs.
    • Tier 4 dungeons no longer list their monster resistances: their hover box already shows too much. The Dungeon Scarab, which makes them, shows them.

    Tags and descriptions¶

    The tag line under the names of identified items got shorter labels, one color per tag, and the game's own stat order.

    Shorter labels

    • allsk is all; replife is rep (red); drain life is drain (red, without the minus sign).
    • Magic items: 30res is 30r, 40life is 40l, 40mana is 40m, as on rares.
    • Maximum resistance is 5%max in the element's color (was 5max). Elemental skill damage on weapons and armor is %f, %l, %c, or %p (was %dmg); jewels and charms keep %dmg in the element's color.
    • Mana regeneration is rgn (was regen); quiver −resistance is -25%r (was %res); jewel requirements are -15%rq (was %req); charm and jewel damage tags drop the +; javelin and bow skill tags too.
    • Damage reduction (gold), minimum and maximum damage (tan), life per kill (red), and mana per kill (blue) keep one color at every value.

    New tags

    • Magic absorb abs, crit chance 10%crit, defense against missiles dvm, Decoy dcoy, Cannot Be Frozen Cbf on rares.
    • Life and mana per character level show as one total at your level.
    • The roll tags of uniques, sets, and runewords add their own: defense def, open wounds ow, element skills (fsk, csk...), auras (gold), single skills (3wcry, 3smas, in tan).
    • Corruptions: Rep (repair durability), half freeze duration, curse duration. Indestructible items don't get Rep; the attack rating corruption shows only its attack rating; the demon and undead corruptions only their damage.
    • A belt or shield with the new single-element maximum resistance affix shows it in the element's color (3%max). With the all maximum resistances corruption as well, it shows 2max for all four and the element's total beside it.

    Highlights

    • Rare body armor with +2 class skills gets a highlight (+2/30fcr), and the circlet, amulet, gloves, shield, and armor highlights have new texts.
    • +Skill highlights (the +5 Fire Golem style description lines) use a blue and teal frame.
    • Damage reduction highlights moved up: the middle band is 8 to 17% (was 8 to 14%), the top band starts at 18% (was 15%), purple at 25% (was 24%).

    Moved or trimmed

    • The sell value $ is the left-most tag of the affix line, not a prefix of the name.
    • Magic attribute tags (str, dex, vit, nrg) don't show on socketed items, where gems change those stats.
    • Corrupted magic class items don't show staffmods. A stat that gives several skills lists the highest first, as the game does.
    • Unique and set amulet and circlet faster cast rate tags are teal up to 10 and purple from 11, as on rares.
    • The All Resistances, Triple Resist, and Dual Resist description lines stop at character level 80 and on filter levels 5 and up.
    • Crafted Affix Level notes: belts count from 80 (was 71), and helms get the note from 65.

    Gold, potions, gems, runes, and PD2 items¶

    Nothing is hidden in town any more: items dropped in town (or a Guild Hall) always show, so trades and stash clean-ups never lose an item.

    • In town, potions, keys, and scrolls that the filter hides in the field show a short label instead (Stam, A, T, Keys x 5, Strangling...).
    • Potions: level 0 uses the same potion looks as the other levels (- H1 -, • H2 •, ( H4 )...). The Light and regular Healing and Mana Potions hide a few character levels later than before.
    • Gold: the higher gold hiding of Endgame Filtering also applies to Advanced Filtering (levels 5 to 12).
    • Gems: every gem stack shows its count; single flawless gems hide on levels 11 and 12 as before. Flawless Topaz now notifies like the other flawless gems.
    • Runes: stacks keep their runes' looks and sounds; BIG GG and LIL GG work with stacks; rune descriptions list the Season 14 runewords.
    • PD2 items: stacks of the boss items and of Vials, Mirrors, Tokens, and Talismans show plural names with their count. Larzuk's Puzzlebox has new colors (blue, orange, yellow, tan), and high runes and the best PD2 items have a new minimap icon center.
    • The Horadric Scroll shows in town.

    Fixes¶

    These Season 13 mistakes are fixed; most were found by the new website pages, which read every rule.

    • Set drop sounds: the High value set items now play their drop sound. In Season 13 the rule checked for uniques by mistake, so set items stayed silent and Valkyrie Wing played the set sound instead.
    • Scepters: non-magical scepters with +3 Conviction or Holy Fire (0 or 4 sockets), or the Doom skills (0 or 5 sockets), were hidden unless they also had Holy Bolt and Fist of the Heavens.
    • Shop helms: maximum damage, minimum damage, life leech, or mana leech each tag a shop helm again.
    • Hidden items with minimap icons: Plague orbs, Leaf staves, ethereal War Darts, and some Phase Blades were hidden while their minimap icon still showed. They now show, or lose the icon.
    • Neophyte claws: no 0-socket claw was ever recognized as a Neophyte base; now Katar, Wrist Blade, Hatchet Hands, Cestus, and the low claws below item level 26 are.
    • Wildspeaker and Pierre Tombale Couant were tiered on the wrong bases; they now use Lion Helm and Partizan.
    • Flawless Topaz got no minimap icon or chat notification.
    • Spiked Shield corruption notes said 2 sockets; they say 3.
    • Ral Rune's crafting note read "Ral Rune Rune".
    • Set rings with the faster cast rate corruption could show fcr twice.
    • Long labels that the game cut off (Stone Crusher's revealed name, several runeword and unique labels) now fit.
