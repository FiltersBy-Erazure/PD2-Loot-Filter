Erazure Filter Levels

How to read it

What each filter level of the Erazure loot filter shows, notifies and hides, for every kind of item that drops, read from the filter's own rules. Pick your level in the launcher, or in game with the filter level hotkeys; the Horadric Cube's description says which level you are on.

  • Each column is a group of filter levels that behave the same; levels that only hide different potions share one (Super Healing H5, Super Mana M5, Rejuvenation 35%).
  • Many items hide only once your character reaches a level: from 80 means from character level 80. The strip under a cell shows character levels 1 to 99. Type your character level in the bar to see your own.
  • Click a cell for its details: which items, when, and what changes in Normal difficulty and special areas. The rows link to the pages with every base and item.
  • Hidden items still show in town, and unique and set items still show until you've discovered them (Discovery Progress). Level 0 hides nothing.
  • Unique and set items drop unidentified: one row per value tier. Non-magical items: two rows each, plain (no sockets, not superior, no skills) and best case (the most a base can show with any sockets, enhanced defense or damage, or skills); the Non-magical Bases page has every base.
